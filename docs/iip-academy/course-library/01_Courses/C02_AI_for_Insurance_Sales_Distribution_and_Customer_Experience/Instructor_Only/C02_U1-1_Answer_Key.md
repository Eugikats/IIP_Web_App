## Formative practice example

Reply: “Thank you for explaining that you use the motorcycle for deliveries. Delivery use needs confirmation before we can explain applicable terms. May I ask the relevant questions and refer your enquiry to the authorised team?”

Handover: “Fictional enquiry: motorcycle used for deliveries; customer requests an explanation and a reply in this conversation. Eligibility, terms, existing cover and premium remain unknown. Training extract C02-SYN-01 requires referral for delivery use. Assigned officer to clarify required details and refer through the approved process; no cover confirmation has been given.”

Accept other clear, supported replies. Check for unsupported cover or payment promises, missing unknowns, unnecessary personal details and absent ownership. This is a teaching answer, not insurer-approved sales wording.
