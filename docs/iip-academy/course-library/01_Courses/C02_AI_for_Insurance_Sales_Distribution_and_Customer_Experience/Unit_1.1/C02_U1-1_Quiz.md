# C02 · Unit 1.1 — Learner quiz

Choose one best answer for each question. Ten questions, one mark each. Proposed pass mark: eight. Complete independently with the AI assistant unavailable. Examples are fictional and use only this unit's training facts.

1. Which sequence best represents the journey taught in this unit?
   - A. Enquiry → discovery → explanation → referral/application → service → renewal review
   - B. Enquiry → acceptance → discovery → guaranteed claim payment
   - C. Renewal → pricing decision → enquiry → automatic acceptance

2. The customer says, “I use a motorcycle for deliveries. Can you explain what cover I might need? Please reply here.” Which fact is established?
   - A. The insurer has accepted delivery use.
   - B. The customer asked for an explanation in this conversation.
   - C. A premium has been agreed.

3. A draft says, “All claims will be paid,” but the source contains no such statement. What should the reviewer do?
   - A. Send it because customers prefer certainty.
   - B. Add “AI-generated” and send it unchanged.
   - C. Remove the unsupported promise and use supported wording or refer the question.

4. The only training extract says delivery use must be referred for confirmation. Which reply fits that evidence?
   - A. “Delivery use needs confirmation; may I clarify the details before referral?”
   - B. “Delivery use is automatically included.”
   - C. “Your cover is active from this message.”

5. A high follow-up priority means:
   - A. The application must be accepted.
   - B. The enquiry merits attention under the prioritisation method; eligibility remains a separate question.
   - C. The customer is guaranteed a lower premium.

6. What makes a human review meaningful?
   - A. A signature added automatically after a fixed delay.
   - B. A reviewer who sees only the final draft and must approve.
   - C. An authorised reviewer with evidence and the ability to edit, return, stop or refer.

7. Which handover best helps the next officer?
   - A. Stated need, known facts, unknowns, source/version, contact status, next action and owner.
   - B. “Hot lead — sell quickly.”
   - C. A numerical score without explanation or source.

8. The customer asks to stop outreach while another draft is queued. What is the appropriate action in the taught workflow?
   - A. Send the queued draft because it was prepared earlier.
   - B. Record the request and stop the relevant workflow under the organisation's procedure.
   - C. Change channel and try again without reviewing the request.

9. Why is “ten completed applications” alone insufficient to compare two pilots?
   - A. Application counts are never useful.
   - B. All pilots must use the same software vendor.
   - C. The period, eligible population, denominator and quality of outcomes are needed.

10. Which statement correctly describes this lesson's diagrams and examples?
   - A. They teach a proposed workflow and do not establish that every capability is live in IIP.
   - B. They prove the platform has integrated every insurer.
   - C. They confirm the fictional product is available for sale.

## Independent practice

Write a three-sentence reply and a short referral note for the motorcycle enquiry. The only product fact supplied is that delivery use needs referral for confirmation. Do not invent benefits, premiums or acceptance. This exercise is formative; the quiz mark is calculated separately.
