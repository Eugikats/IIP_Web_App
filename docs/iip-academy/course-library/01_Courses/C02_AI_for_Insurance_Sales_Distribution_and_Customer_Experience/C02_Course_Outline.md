# C02 — AI for Insurance Sales, Distribution & Customer Experience

Version 1 · 18 October 2026 · editorial draft

## Audience and purpose

For insurance agents, bancassurance officers, relationship managers, brokers and customer service teams. Learners practise using AI to prepare relevant enquiries, follow-up and service work while retaining responsibility for what customers are told. Examples use fictional customers and fictional products; nothing is an offer of insurance.

Prior knowledge: everyday customer-facing insurance work. Recommended foundation: C01 Unit 1.1 on rules, machine learning, generative AI and agentic AI. C02 can be taught independently with a short terminology recap.

## Course outcomes

By the end, learners should be able to:

1. Map an insurance enquiry from first contact to referral, service and renewal, assigning an owner to each step.
2. Separate customer statements, source facts, unknowns and AI suggestions.
3. Prepare a clear, source-grounded customer response and a useful human handover.
4. Explain a follow-up priority using relevant evidence without treating a score as eligibility or a decision.
5. Set review and stop points for AI-assisted outreach and service workflows.
6. Evaluate customer experience alongside commercial outcomes using a defined denominator and period.

## Assessment design

- Unit quizzes: ten questions after each unit; only Unit 1.1's questions are authored in this pack.
- Module assessments: fifteen questions spanning all three units; not yet authored.
- End-of-course practical task: apply the full workflow to a fictional branch or agency enquiry, outreach, service and renewal case. Learner must produce discovery notes, sources, checked messages, a handover, a stop/escalation map and a measurement plan.
- Proposed rubric, 100 marks: customer evidence and unknowns 20; source grounding and message accuracy 25; ownership and review controls 25; handover and customer clarity 15; measurement and pilot plan 15.
- Proposed pass thresholds: 8/10 per unit, 12/15 per module, 70/100 for the capstone. These are editorial proposals, not existing system settings.
- A claim that cover is confirmed without evidence, an invented benefit or an ignored stop request requires correction before completion, regardless of the numerical mark.
- Hide the AI assistant during quizzes, module assessments and the capstone. Enforce access on the server; hiding the bubble alone is insufficient. Keep keys and rubric guidance restricted to authorised assessors. Do not make assessment answers available to a learner-facing retrieval assistant.
- Award wording: certificate of completion. No accreditation claim.

## Delivery boundaries

The training describes a proposed way of working. It does not assert that every demonstrated function is currently connected in IIP. Use a verified screen or a clearly labelled teaching diagram. Confirm the current approved product source and local organisational procedures before a learner-facing release. No insurer pricing, regulatory deadlines, product recommendations or provider cost estimates are taught in this starter lesson.
