## 1. AI & Agentic AI for Insurance

`ai-agentic-insurance` · AI foundations · Introductory · 12 units in 4 modules, with a final applied task · Ready to start

Foundations of AI, generative AI and agentic AI across the insurance value chain, taught practically through IIP.

On completing this course, an insurance professional working in Uganda, a claims officer, underwriter, broker, bancassurance relationship manager or customer service officer, will be able to identify where AI and agentic AI can add value in their own work, use generative AI tools safely and productively, verify AI output before relying on it, protect personal data in line with the Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019, and assess an AI use case against recognised governance principles so that it can be proposed responsibly to their organisation.

**Who it is for:** Insurance and bancassurance leaders, heads of claims, underwriting and distribution, compliance and risk officers, and anyone who must judge AI before buying or running it

**Roles:**
- Claims officers, underwriters, brokers, bancassurance relationship managers and customer service officers
- People with working knowledge of insurance operations and no technical AI background

**By the end you can:**
- Identify where AI and agentic AI can add value in your own work
- Use generative AI tools safely and productively
- Verify AI output before relying on it
- Protect personal data in line with the Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019
- Assess an AI use case against recognised governance principles so it can be proposed responsibly

**Parts of IIP it uses:** Command Center, Inbox, Claims, Data Hub, Audit

**To earn the certificate:** Pass all twelve unit quizzes (70%), all four module assessments (70%, up to three attempts each) and the final applied task (60 of 100).

**Content status:** Pilot content. Written from desk research. Review by a subject expert and checks against practitioner interviews and the full text of the laws cited are still to be done.

### Final applied task

**AI Use-Case Proposal and Governance Review**. Pass mark 60 of 100, marked by a person. Proposal of 1500 to 2000 words plus a 10-box canvas.

Choose one real process in your own function (claims, underwriting, distribution/bancassurance, broking, customer service, compliance or finance) where AI or agentic AI could help. Prepare a proposal your manager or risk committee could act on. Use anonymised or illustrative data only, do not include any real customer personal data.

**What to hand in:** A completed AI Use-Case Assessment Canvas (all ten boxes), and a written proposal of 1,500–2,000 words covering: the problem and baseline; the proposed solution and its AI category; a decision-rights matrix with autonomy levels per action; a data protection analysis under the DPPA 2019; a risk-and-control table; a mapping to the IAIS five topics and NIST four functions; a pilot plan with success metrics, owner and review date; and two example prompts or agent instructions the solution would use, each with the verification step that would follow.

Canvas boxes: 1. Problem and baseline; 2. Proposed AI solution; 3. Users and affected people; 4. Data; 5. Autonomy and decision rights; 6. Value; 7. Risks; 8. Controls; 9. Governance mapping; 10. Recommendation and pilot plan

| Criterion | Marks | What full marks looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Problem definition and baseline | 10 | Specific, quantified, clearly linked to business or customer outcomes |
| Solution design and AI classification | 10 | Correct category; realistic about Ugandan data, documents and connectivity |
| Decision rights and human oversight | 15 | Per-action autonomy levels; meaningful escalation triggers; audit and stop mechanism |
| Data protection analysis | 15 | Correct application of DPPA principles, minimisation, cross-border processing and automated decision rights |
| Risk identification and controls | 15 | Covers fairness, explainability, security, third-party, conduct and over-reliance with matched controls |
| Governance mapping | 10 | Accurate use of IAIS topics and NIST functions, not merely listed |
| Pilot plan and metrics | 10 | Scoped, time-bound, measurable, with owner and review date |
| Prompts/instructions and verification | 10 | Structured (RCTCF), grounded, with an appropriate 3C verification step |
| Professional quality | 5 | Clear, concise, ready for a manager |

**Integrity:** Each learner's process, organisation type and baseline differ, which makes copied or generic AI-written submissions easy to spot. Markers check for consistency between the canvas and the narrative, specific local detail, and realistic numbers. Submissions may be followed by a short oral check (10 minutes) at the marker's discretion.
