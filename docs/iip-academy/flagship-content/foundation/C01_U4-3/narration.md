### SLIDE 03 - Core concepts and terminology

**On-screen text:** It has ten boxes: (1) Problem and baseline : what is going wrong today, in numbers; (2) Proposed AI solution : category (rules, ML, GenAI, agent) and what...

**Visual:** Editable teaching text and an insurance example; no invented system screenshot.

NARRATION:
It has ten boxes: (1) Problem and baseline : what is going wrong today, in numbers; (2) Proposed AI solution : category (rules, ML, GenAI, agent) and what it does; (3) Users and affected people : staff who use it, customers affected; (4) Data : what data, whose, where it is processed, DPPA basis; (5) Autonomy and decision rights : level per action, what is never automated; (6) Value : expected benefit and how it will be measured; (7) Risks : fairness, explainability, security, third-party, conduct, over-reliance; (8) Controls : verification steps, escalation triggers, audit trail, monitoring, stop mechanism; (9) Governance mapping : IAIS topics and NIST functions addressed; (10) Recommendation and pilot plan : proceed, proceed with conditions, or do not proceed; scope, duration, success criteria, owner, review date.
END NARRATION

### SLIDE 05 - Worked example (for video)

**On-screen text:** Use case: WhatsApp claims-intake agent at a Ugandan general insurer.

**Visual:** Editable teaching text and an insurance example; no invented system screenshot.

NARRATION:
Use case: WhatsApp claims-intake agent at a Ugandan general insurer. Problem: files take too long to complete because documents are missing; baseline average days from notification to complete file recorded over three months. Solution: agent at Level 2 to 3 for checklists and reminders, Level 0 for decisions. Data: names, phone numbers, policy numbers, vehicle registration; processed by a vendor : data location and terms reviewed with DPO. Risks: misreading documents, prompt injection via uploaded files, tone with distressed customers. Controls: handwritten documents routed to staff; no payment or decline authority; escalation on death claims, complaints, and threshold amounts; weekly 5% sample review; kill switch held by claims manager. Recommendation: proceed with a three-month pilot in one branch; success = fewer days to complete file with no rise in complaints.
END NARRATION

### SLIDE 07 - Practise, check and apply

**On-screen text:** Complete the practice task · Record your reasoning · Take the ten-question quiz independently

**Visual:** Editable teaching text and an insurance example; no invented system screenshot.

NARRATION:
Before taking the quiz, write down the main idea in your own words. Apply it to a fictional task in your role, name the evidence needed and identify what you would check before anyone relies on the result. Keep unresolved questions visible and route decisions to the authorised person. Complete the ten-question quiz independently, then revisit any concept that needs more practice.
END NARRATION
