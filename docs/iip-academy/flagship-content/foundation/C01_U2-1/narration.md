### SLIDE 02 - Core concepts and terminology

**On-screen text:** A prompt is the instruction and material given to an AI model.

**Visual:** Editable teaching text and an insurance example; no invented system screenshot.

NARRATION:
A prompt is the instruction and material given to an AI model. Because the model generates the most plausible continuation of what it receives, vague prompts get generic answers and well-structured prompts get specific, usable ones. The Academy teaches a five-part structure, RCTCF: Role (who the AI should act as, e.g., "a claims communication specialist at a Ugandan general insurer"), Context (the facts of the situation, the audience and any source material), Task (exactly what to produce), Constraints (what to avoid, length, tone, what must be included, "use only the text provided"), and Format (letter, table, bullet summary, WhatsApp message under 300 characters). Two further techniques lift quality: giving an example of the desired output, and asking the model to ask clarifying questions before drafting.
END NARRATION

### SLIDE 04 - Core concepts and terminology

**On-screen text:** Prompting is not a way to make the model accurate about facts it does not have.

**Visual:** Editable teaching text and an insurance example; no invented system screenshot.

NARRATION:
Prompting is not a way to make the model accurate about facts it does not have. It improves structure, tone and relevance; accuracy still depends on providing correct source information and verifying the result (Unit 2.2).
END NARRATION

### SLIDE 05 - Realistic Ugandan scenarios

**On-screen text:** Weak prompt: "Write a letter about a delayed claim." Strong prompt: "You are a claims communication specialist at a Ugandan general insurer.

**Visual:** Editable teaching text and an insurance example; no invented system screenshot.

NARRATION:
Weak prompt: "Write a letter about a delayed claim." Strong prompt: "You are a claims communication specialist at a Ugandan general insurer. A customer's motor own-damage claim is delayed because the police abstract has not been received. Write a WhatsApp message under 120 words, in plain English, that apologises for the wait, explains exactly which document is outstanding and where to get it, gives the claims officer's direct line, and does not promise a settlement date. Use a respectful, warm tone."
END NARRATION

### SLIDE 11 - Practise, check and apply

**Visual:** Editable teaching text and an insurance example; no invented system screenshot.

NARRATION:
Before taking the quiz, write down the main idea in your own words. Apply it to a fictional task in your role, name the evidence needed and identify what you would check before anyone relies on the result.
