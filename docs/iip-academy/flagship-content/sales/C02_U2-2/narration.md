# C02_U2-2: Preparing a relevant outreach message

Authored teaching draft. Fictional scenarios, not actual policy advice. Source and expert review remain part of release checks.

### SLIDE 01 - Preparing a relevant outreach message

**On-screen text:** Understand the task · Work through a fictional case · Practise and check your reasoning

**Visual:** Editable teaching content; emphasise the method or fictional case rather than a fabricated system screenshot.

NARRATION:
Welcome to Preparing a relevant outreach message. The learning goal is to draft a useful, accurate invitation with an appropriate channel and a clear next step. We will connect the method to a fictional insurance scenario, then ask you to prepare a work product you can check. The examples are not insurer product advice and do not establish that any feature is live.
END NARRATION

### SLIDE 02 - Set audience and purpose

**On-screen text:** Identify the intended audience and the single action the message should support. · Personalisation should be relevant and proportionate; avoid inserting sensitive or unexpected details just...

**Visual:** Editable teaching content; emphasise the method or fictional case rather than a fabricated system screenshot.

NARRATION:
Identify the intended audience and the single action the message should support. An invitation to ask about cover is different from an application request or a policy notice. Use only authorised audience inputs. Personalisation should be relevant and proportionate; avoid inserting sensitive or unexpected details just because a system can access them.
END NARRATION

### SLIDE 03 - Provide a bounded brief

**On-screen text:** Tell the drafting tool who the message is for, its purpose, the approved... · Do not ask the tool to invent urgency, a discount, a premium or...

**Visual:** Editable teaching content; emphasise the method or fictional case rather than a fabricated system screenshot.

NARRATION:
Tell the drafting tool who the message is for, its purpose, the approved product source, the channel, the permitted tone and what it must not claim. Ask for a short draft and separate unresolved questions. Do not ask the tool to invent urgency, a discount, a premium or a customer's insurance status.
END NARRATION

### SLIDE 04 - Make the next step easy

**On-screen text:** Use plain language and one clear next step, such as asking a question... · Channel-specific templates and restrictions must be checked by the channel owner before sending.

**Visual:** Editable teaching content; emphasise the method or fictional case rather than a fabricated system screenshot.

NARRATION:
Use plain language and one clear next step, such as asking a question or requesting a discussion with an officer. Identify the organisation appropriately and avoid implying cover already exists. Include the organisation's required contact-choice wording. Channel-specific templates and restrictions must be checked by the channel owner before sending.
END NARRATION

### SLIDE 05 - Approve the exact draft

**On-screen text:** The reviewer sees the intended audience, source, contact status and exact version to... · A prepared draft is not a delivered message, and a failed send must...

**Visual:** Editable teaching content; emphasise the method or fictional case rather than a fabricated system screenshot.

NARRATION:
The reviewer sees the intended audience, source, contact status and exact version to be sent. An approval for one draft must not be treated as approval for a materially changed message. Keep the approval and send result recorded. A prepared draft is not a delivered message, and a failed send must not be shown as customer engagement.
END NARRATION

### SLIDE 06 - Work through the fictional case

**On-screen text:** A draft says, 'Your renewal discount expires tonight,' but no approved discount or deadline exists. Remove the invented urgency. If the purpose is a discussion of an upcoming verified renewal, state that...

**Visual:** Editable teaching content; emphasise the method or fictional case rather than a fabricated system screenshot.

NARRATION:
A draft says, 'Your renewal discount expires tonight,' but no approved discount or deadline exists. Remove the invented urgency. If the purpose is a discussion of an upcoming verified renewal, state that purpose and invite the customer to review their needs with an officer. The review record captures the approved wording and audience before any send.
END NARRATION

### SLIDE 07 - Prepare the practical work

**On-screen text:** Write a bounded drafting brief and a message of no more than seventy words. Mark the source, contact-status check, reviewer and intended next step separately from the customer message.

**Visual:** Editable teaching content; emphasise the method or fictional case rather than a fabricated system screenshot.

NARRATION:
Write a bounded drafting brief and a message of no more than seventy words. Mark the source, contact-status check, reviewer and intended next step separately from the customer message. Pause the lesson and create the work in your own words. Use fictional information. Where a source or fact is missing, mark it as unresolved rather than filling the gap with an assumption. Keep your first draft so that you can compare it with the checked version.
END NARRATION

### SLIDE 08 - Review your work

**On-screen text:** Evidence · Meaning · Recipient · Authority · Next action

**Visual:** Editable teaching content; emphasise the method or fictional case rather than a fabricated system screenshot.

NARRATION:
Review your work in five passes. First, check each factual statement against the information supplied. Second, check that simplified wording has not changed the meaning. Third, check the intended recipient and whether the information is appropriate for them. Fourth, identify the authority required for any consequential action. Finally, make sure the next step has an owner and that unresolved questions remain visible. Record a correction you made and why it matters to the customer.
END NARRATION

### SLIDE 09 - Compare an unsafe and a useful response

**On-screen text:** Unsupported certainty hides a gap · A checked next step makes the gap manageable

**Visual:** Editable teaching content; emphasise the method or fictional case rather than a fabricated system screenshot.

NARRATION:
An unsafe work product can look complete because it substitutes certainty for missing evidence. A useful one says what is known, what remains unconfirmed and what an authorised person should do next. Apply that distinction to your own draft. If you have written that a policy is active, a price is final, a claim will be paid or an improvement is proven, identify the record that supports that statement. Remove or refer any conclusion that the source does not establish.
END NARRATION

### SLIDE 10 - Your takeaways and independent quiz

**Visual:** Editable teaching content; emphasise the method or fictional case rather than a fabricated system screenshot.
