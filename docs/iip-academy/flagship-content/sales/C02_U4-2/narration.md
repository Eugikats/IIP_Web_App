### SLIDE 02 - Define the question first

**On-screen text:** Decide what the team wants to learn, such as whether a preparation step... · Choose measures that correspond to the intended effect rather than whichever number is...

**Visual:** Editable teaching content; emphasise the method or fictional case rather than a fabricated system screenshot.

NARRATION:
Decide what the team wants to learn, such as whether a preparation step reduces incomplete handovers without worsening customer confusion. Record the population and time period. A count of messages does not answer that question. Choose measures that correspond to the intended effect rather than whichever number is easiest to collect.
END NARRATION

### SLIDE 03 - Use a denominator

**On-screen text:** A conversion rate is completed outcomes divided by the defined eligible population, not... · Do not compare this directly with a team counting all inbound contacts as...

**Visual:** Editable teaching content; emphasise the method or fictional case rather than a fabricated system screenshot.

NARRATION:
A conversion rate is completed outcomes divided by the defined eligible population, not an isolated total. In a fictional example, twelve completed applications out of sixty qualified enquiries is twenty per cent. State the period, definitions and exclusions. Do not compare this directly with a team counting all inbound contacts as its denominator.
END NARRATION

### SLIDE 04 - Measure quality and potential harm

**On-screen text:** Track material corrections, unsupported statements caught, unanswered handovers, complaints and whether stop requests... · Protect access to customer-level records used for measurement.

**Visual:** Editable teaching content; emphasise the method or fictional case rather than a fabricated system screenshot.

NARRATION:
Track material corrections, unsupported statements caught, unanswered handovers, complaints and whether stop requests were honoured. Define what counts as an error and how samples are reviewed. A higher application count accompanied by misleading messages is not evidence of an acceptable improvement. Protect access to customer-level records used for measurement.
END NARRATION

### SLIDE 05 - Interpret comparison carefully

**On-screen text:** Compare similar populations and periods and record changes in staffing, campaigns or product... · Decide what result would justify continuing, revising or stopping before examining the final...

**Visual:** Editable teaching content; emphasise the method or fictional case rather than a fabricated system screenshot.

NARRATION:
Compare similar populations and periods and record changes in staffing, campaigns or product availability. A small pilot can suggest a pattern without proving causation. Show sample size, missing data and uncertainty. Decide what result would justify continuing, revising or stopping before examining the final figures, and retain the reasons for the decision.
END NARRATION

### SLIDE 06 - Work through the fictional case

**On-screen text:** A team reports that applications rose from ten to twelve. The earlier period had twenty qualified enquiries and the later period sixty. The counts increased, but the defined conversion rates moved from...

**Visual:** Editable teaching content; emphasise the method or fictional case rather than a fabricated system screenshot.

NARRATION:
A team reports that applications rose from ten to twelve. The earlier period had twenty qualified enquiries and the later period sixty. The counts increased, but the defined conversion rates moved from fifty to twenty per cent. Neither alone explains why. Review population differences, source changes and quality measures before claiming that AI improved performance.
END NARRATION

### SLIDE 07 - Prepare the practical work

**On-screen text:** Create a measurement sheet with metric, definition, numerator, denominator, period, data source, owner and review action. Calculate the two fictional rates and explain the limits of the comparison.

**Visual:** Editable teaching content; emphasise the method or fictional case rather than a fabricated system screenshot.

NARRATION:
Create a measurement sheet with metric, definition, numerator, denominator, period, data source, owner and review action. Calculate the two fictional rates and explain the limits of the comparison. Pause the lesson and create the work in your own words. Use fictional information. Where a source or fact is missing, mark it as unresolved rather than filling the gap with an assumption. Keep your first draft so that you can compare it with the checked version.
END NARRATION

### SLIDE 08 - Review your work

**On-screen text:** Evidence · Meaning · Recipient · Authority · Next action

**Visual:** Editable teaching content; emphasise the method or fictional case rather than a fabricated system screenshot.

NARRATION:
Review your work in five passes. First, check each factual statement against the information supplied. Second, check that simplified wording has not changed the meaning. Third, check the intended recipient and whether the information is appropriate for them. Fourth, identify the authority required for any consequential action. Finally, make sure the next step has an owner and that unresolved questions remain visible. Record a correction you made and why it matters to the customer.
END NARRATION

### SLIDE 09 - Compare an unsafe and a useful response

**On-screen text:** Unsupported certainty hides a gap · A checked next step makes the gap manageable

**Visual:** Editable teaching content; emphasise the method or fictional case rather than a fabricated system screenshot.

NARRATION:
An unsafe work product can look complete because it substitutes certainty for missing evidence. A useful one says what is known, what remains unconfirmed and what an authorised person should do next. Apply that distinction to your own draft. If you have written that a policy is active, a price is final, a claim will be paid or an improvement is proven, identify the record that supports that statement. Remove or refer any conclusion that the source does not establish.
END NARRATION
