NARRATION:
Welcome to Lead qualification and prioritisation. The learning goal is to choose the next follow-up using evidence without treating a worklist score as insurance eligibility. We will connect the method to a fictional insurance scenario, then ask you to prepare a work product you can check. The examples are not insurer product advice and do not establish that any feature is live.

### SLIDE 02 - Define the next action

**On-screen text:** Qualification asks whether there is enough relevant information and interest for a particular... · A record can be ready for clarification while still being unready for an...

**Visual:** Editable teaching content; emphasise the method or fictional case rather than a fabricated system screenshot.

NARRATION:
Qualification asks whether there is enough relevant information and interest for a particular next step. It is not a promise that an insurer will accept the customer. Define the action first: clarify a missing fact, explain an option or refer an application. A record can be ready for clarification while still being unready for an application.
END NARRATION

### SLIDE 03 - Use relevant evidence

**On-screen text:** A priority can reflect a stated need, requested timing, required missing information and... · Follow the approved method and flag missing information rather than using a precise-looking...

**Visual:** Editable teaching content; emphasise the method or fictional case rather than a fabricated system screenshot.

NARRATION:
A priority can reflect a stated need, requested timing, required missing information and the current contact status. Explain the reasons beside the priority. Do not infer affordability, eligibility or interest from an unrelated personal characteristic. Follow the approved method and flag missing information rather than using a precise-looking score to conceal it.
END NARRATION

### SLIDE 04 - Apply contact status first

**On-screen text:** A customer who asked not to receive outreach does not become contactable because... · Escalate unclear status rather than choosing the interpretation most likely to produce a...

**Visual:** Editable teaching content; emphasise the method or fictional case rather than a fabricated system screenshot.

NARRATION:
A customer who asked not to receive outreach does not become contactable because the lead looks valuable. Check contact status and permitted channel before preparing follow-up. Separate marketing follow-up from necessary service work under the organisation's procedure. Escalate unclear status rather than choosing the interpretation most likely to produce a sale.
END NARRATION

### SLIDE 05 - Review the queue as work

**On-screen text:** A useful queue has a reason, owner and next action, not just a... · A low follow-up priority is not a rejection of cover.

**Visual:** Editable teaching content; emphasise the method or fictional case rather than a fabricated system screenshot.

NARRATION:
A useful queue has a reason, owner and next action, not just a ranking. Review stale records, duplicated enquiries and changes in customer circumstances. Record why a priority changed. The officer can correct the ranking when the evidence changes, and the correction should remain traceable. A low follow-up priority is not a rejection of cover.
END NARRATION

### SLIDE 06 - Work through the fictional case

**On-screen text:** Three fictional enquiries arrive. One asks for a callback today with the relevant details supplied. Another shows strong interest but asks to stop marketing contact. A third asks a general question and...

**Visual:** Editable teaching content; emphasise the method or fictional case rather than a fabricated system screenshot.

NARRATION:
Three fictional enquiries arrive. One asks for a callback today with the relevant details supplied. Another shows strong interest but asks to stop marketing contact. A third asks a general question and has not supplied the information needed for referral. Contact status prevents outreach to the second. Assign the first callback and prepare a clarification response for the third, using the authorised channel and review process.
END NARRATION

### SLIDE 07 - Prepare the practical work

**On-screen text:** Create a worklist with evidence, missing facts, contact status, owner and next action. Explain why a priority is not an underwriting decision.

**Visual:** Editable teaching content; emphasise the method or fictional case rather than a fabricated system screenshot.

NARRATION:
Create a worklist with evidence, missing facts, contact status, owner and next action. Explain why a priority is not an underwriting decision. Pause the lesson and create the work in your own words. Use fictional information. Where a source or fact is missing, mark it as unresolved rather than filling the gap with an assumption. Keep your first draft so that you can compare it with the checked version.
END NARRATION

### SLIDE 08 - Review your work

**On-screen text:** Evidence · Meaning · Recipient · Authority · Next action

**Visual:** Editable teaching content; emphasise the method or fictional case rather than a fabricated system screenshot.

NARRATION:
Review your work in five passes. First, check each factual statement against the information supplied. Second, check that simplified wording has not changed the meaning. Third, check the intended recipient and whether the information is appropriate for them. Fourth, identify the authority required for any consequential action. Finally, make sure the next step has an owner and that unresolved questions remain visible. Record a correction you made and why it matters to the customer.
END NARRATION

### SLIDE 09 - Compare an unsafe and a useful response

**On-screen text:** Unsupported certainty hides a gap · A checked next step makes the gap manageable

**Visual:** Editable teaching content; emphasise the method or fictional case rather than a fabricated system screenshot.

NARRATION:
An unsafe work product can look complete because it substitutes certainty for missing evidence. A useful one says what is known, what remains unconfirmed and what an authorised person should do next. Apply that distinction to your own draft. If you have written that a policy is active, a price is final, a claim will be paid or an improvement is proven, identify the record that supports that statement. Remove or refer any conclusion that the source does not establish.
END NARRATION
