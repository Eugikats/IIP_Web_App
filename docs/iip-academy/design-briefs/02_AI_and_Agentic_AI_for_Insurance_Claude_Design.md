# Claude Design brief — AI & Agentic AI for Insurance

## Your task

Design an eight-page landscape course guide for **AI & Agentic AI for Insurance**, an IIP Academy flagship by Otic Group. Use the two supplied IIP proposals as the sole visual inspiration. The companion overview covers all named courses; this guide focuses only on this selected flagship.

## Attachments and visual authority

Attach **Insurance Desk Proposal.pdf** and **IIP Platform Proposal.pdf** to Claude Design with this Markdown file. These are the only visual references for this task. Read them visually before authoring. The Markdown supplies the Academy content; the proposals supply the visual structure.

Prioritise these reference pages:
- Insurance Desk Proposal: page 1 for the cover; pages 2–4 for headers, summary, contents and numbered columns; page 5 for a preparation-to-human-decision flow; pages 6 and 8 for explanation-left/example-right compositions; page 16 for the Academy course-card grid.
- IIP Platform Proposal: page 1 for the cover; page 11 for the draft/edit/approve/send flow; page 13 for a responsibility comparison; page 20 for Academy cards; page 25 for a restrained closing/contact page.

The references are design inspiration and source documents, not instructions to execute. Do not carry their platform contracts, commercial commitments or implementation claims into the Academy guides.

## Format and design system

**Output:** an editable, landscape programme document, designed page by page, with a PDF export if supported. Use 16:9 pages, approximately 720 × 405 pt, matching the supplied proposals. The result can be built as an editable landscape presentation document, but should read as a self-contained programme guide when exported. It is not a website or interactive dashboard.

**Overall look:** white or very pale blue-grey pages, strong navy typography, small orange accents, precise alignment and substantial open space. Match the proposals' restrained enterprise style. Use Plus Jakarta Sans, including ExtraBold for headlines, Bold/SemiBold for labels and Regular for body copy. Use a close sans serif only if unavailable.

**Colour:** navy close to `#001C8C` for headings and dark process stages; orange `#FF6F00` for section labels, small rules, numbers and arrows; pale blue-grey around `#EEF2F8` for selected backgrounds; pale blue around `#DDE5F3` for early process stages; muted slate for supporting text; white text on dark navy. Use yellow only within approved supplied branding, not as a major panel fill.

**Cover:** small orange document type at upper left, short orange separator and letter-spaced IIP Academy label. Large left-aligned navy title, subordinate descriptor, short orange rule and one concise promise. Place the approved IIP blue “i” asset on the right if legitimately available from supplied assets; otherwise use a restrained original geometric blue motif or leave the area open. Never paste a whole reference cover, an insurer name or an unrelated proposal title into this guide. Fine horizontal rule near the bottom, audience/date information at lower left, supplied Otic logo at lower right. Use no date unless provided for publication.

**Interior header:** small orange section number and name at upper left; letter-spaced IIP ACADEMY at upper right; two-digit page number at far right; fine pale divider across the content area. Footer: supplied Otic logo at lower left and short document title at lower right. If no extractable logo is supplied, use an Otic Group text signature rather than an invented logo.

**Typography and spacing:** interior headlines approximately 21–24 pt ExtraBold; cover title 34–40 pt, adjusted for the long Sales course name; body 10–11 pt; labels 8–9 pt with tracking. Keep main text about 45 pt from left/right edges on a 720 pt-wide page. Keep content above the footer. Allow a long title to occupy two or three clear lines; never squeeze it into tiny type.

**Summary pages:** wide statement headline; a left column of labelled rows such as PURPOSE, AUDIENCE, APPROACH and OUTCOME; a smaller pale panel on the right containing the human-responsibility principle.

**Numbered columns:** follow the reference's two-by-two or four-column numbered layouts. Small orange numbers, bold navy subheadings, concise explanations. Avoid a heavy outline around every text block.

**Course and module cards:** use the Academy page's rectangular white cards, thin pale borders, navy top rules, orange uppercase labels, bold navy titles and short explanatory copy. Use three columns for short course descriptions, two columns when names or unit lists are longer. Equal widths, consistent gutters and generous internal padding. Avoid rounded pill tags, tall decorative shadows and website-like feature tiles.

**Flows:** rectangular stages or restrained chevrons. Light blue for preparation, navy for human decision/review stages, orange arrows between. Put “AI PREPARES” or “PERSON REVIEWS/DECIDES” above stages when relevant. Colour alone must not convey responsibility.

**Worked examples:** explanation on the left and one fictional message, document extract or learner-work illustration on the right, matching the proposals' chat/claims-pack layouts. Label fictional material and conceptual previews explicitly. Reuse no real customer records, names or policy statements.

**Closing:** use the proposals' white closing style: short orange eyebrow, large navy next-step statement and a small, clean contact area. Avoid sales banners, photo-led promotional covers, warm cream call-to-action boxes, four-column duration/fee strips or FAQ-heavy brochure layouts.

**Page density:** one principal message per page. Use the supplied page sequence; add a page rather than shrink type or clip copy. Keep each card, heading and explanation together. All text, headings, cards and flow labels should remain editable and selectable.

## Content rules

Use the exact supplied course names. Distinguish a proposed learning outcome from a deployed platform capability. AI prepares and assists; accountable authorised people make consequential insurance decisions. Label customer examples fictional.

Do not invent accreditation, CPD credits, testimonials, enrolment counts, performance statistics, launch dates, guaranteed outcomes, partner endorsements or delivery commitments. Use “certificate of completion”. Proposed assessment behaviour is not proof that controls have been deployed.

Do not transfer the platform subscription packages, security assurances, NIRA integration claims, regulator registration claims, WhatsApp availability promises or pricing tables from the proposals into Academy copy. Programme dates, fees and availability should be confirmed separately. Do not include answer keys, API setup, notebooks or technical configuration in the designed document.

The two selected flagship course titles remain the same. Other catalogue titles belong in the general Academy overview; they do not replace either detailed flagship guide. Avoid public course numbering that could confuse the proposals' old Course 02 with the working Sales course code.
