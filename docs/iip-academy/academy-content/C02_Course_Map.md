# Course 2 — Claims Communication & Service Recovery

## Course map: modules, units and learning outcomes (draft for approval)

### Course outcome

Learners will be able to communicate claims processes, documentation needs, delays and decisions clearly and empathetically; handle frustrated customers professionally; recover from service failures; and use AI responsibly to improve the speed and quality of claims communication — within Uganda's regulatory and data protection requirements.

### Gaps before research packs can be built

IRA complaints-handling guidance and complaints statistics, the Insurance Appeals Tribunal procedure, and recognised service-recovery and de-escalation frameworks still need to be sourced and added to the register. Practitioner and insured-person interviews (S16) are particularly important for this course because its scenarios must reflect real Ugandan claims experiences.
