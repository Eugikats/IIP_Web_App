## 3. Course standard (applies to every course)

Each course is independently purchased (UGX 500,000) and follows the same architecture. A course contains modules; each module contains units. Each unit has one slide-based video (8–12 minutes) followed by a 10-question quiz. Each module closes with a 15-question assessment covering all its units. The course ends with one applied task that resembles real professional work. The Academy AI assistant is available during videos and guided practice and hidden during unit quizzes, module assessments and the final task. Because hiding the assistant does not stop learners using external AI tools, assessments rely on scenario-based questions, question-bank variation and an applied task that requires the learner's own workplace context.

## 5. Arithmetic checks performed

Figures were recomputed in Python before inclusion. Life premium growth (977.6 ÷ 702.25 − 1) = 39.21%, matching IRA. Claims paid as a share of GWP (934.55 ÷ 2,024) = 46.17%, matching IRA's 46.2%. GWP growth from the rounded figures (2.024 ÷ 1.764 − 1) = 14.74%; IRA reports 14.72%, the difference coming from IRA's unrounded base, so lessons should quote IRA's 14.72%. Agentic share in Q2 2026 (6 ÷ 37) = 16.2%, matching Evident's 16%.
