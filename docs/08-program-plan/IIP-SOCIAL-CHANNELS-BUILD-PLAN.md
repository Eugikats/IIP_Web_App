## Goal

Make IIP comfortable to use as an insurer's connected claims operations workspace: link approved external systems to IIP's canonical claims and document records; help staff extract, review, and export evidence; carry verified customer context across channels; route safe communications and operational notifications; and configure supported languages. Keep insurers' human authority over consequential insurance decisions. Keep Azure AI Search as the existing retrieval foundation. Do not treat a connector, AI reviewer, anomaly signal, or autonomous message as authority to make an insurance decision.

For social messaging, allow an insurer workspace to connect an eligible account, receive real customer messages in the existing Inbox, and send only messages allowed by its selected operating mode, tenant permissions, account ownership, consent, and provider rules. Keep channels marked `Not configured` until real capabilities are verified.

### Connector approach

Use **Connectors** as the Settings label: it describes connecting an insurer's existing system and does not imply installing executable plugins. Build a catalogue with provider cards and a guided setup, health, field mapping, test connection, first sync, last successful sync, and disconnect/recovery states.

The recommended architecture has one versioned IIP canonical inbound contract plus provider-specific adapters. A shared endpoint can accept the canonical contract from an insurer-owned middleware or integration partner; adapters translate each vendor's schema and auth model at the boundary. This avoids both a fictitious universal vendor endpoint and a separate domain model per vendor. Support API pull/webhooks first where documented, then controlled CSV/SFTP import for legacy systems. The sync path must be tenant/workspace-bound server-side, idempotent, replayable, observable, and reconcilable; carry source-system IDs and source provenance into IIP. Keep write-back separate, human-approved, and limited to explicitly supported non-final actions.

Start with one pilot insurer's read-only policy, claim and document flow. Extend the current REST policy adapter and file-import path where their contracts fit. Do not build all named vendor adapters before a real customer supplies a contract. Handle legacy on-premises access through an insurer-approved gateway or controlled file exchange; network reachability is a separate requirement from having an API. Restrict configured destinations and redirects, protect credentials, and validate downloaded evidence before indexing it.

| External data | IIP destination and merge rule |
|---|---|
| Customer, insured, claimant, beneficiary and provider IDs | Keep each role distinct. Maintain source-system-to-IIP identity links with verified relationship evidence; a shared name or phone does not merge people. |
| Policy, coverage and wording version | Link to the existing policy and approved product/version. Preserve the authoritative external value and retrieval time; do not infer missing cover. |
| Claim ID, loss date, report date, type and status | Deduplicate by tenant, connection and external claim ID. Preserve external status separately and map to the owning IIP workflow contract; importing status never fabricates a human approval. |

| Estimates, invoices, payments and amounts | Preserve money, currency, line items and amount meaning. Import paid status as external evidence only; it does not authorize IIP payment execution. |
| Updates, deletions and conflicts | Track source version/cursor and deletion events. Repeated sync must not create duplicate cases or overwrite staff corrections. Show conflicting values and their sources for review; document field ownership and reconciliation rules. |

### Fraud and anomaly research

IIP already has a useful deterministic first slice. Uganda IRA's 2023 market report records reported investigations including forged/false documents, motor-related cases, theft, life, property/asset, goods-in-transit, and false information; it also discusses vehicle pre-inspection to avoid insuring already-damaged vehicles. These are reported cases, not prevalence estimates or a model-training set. [IRA 2023 market report](https://ira.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Market-Report-2023.pdf). IRA discussions in 2025 also cite health billing inconsistencies and fraud/abuse; a 2026 meeting describes shared fraud-data work as proposed/under development, so IIP must not assume access to an industry-wide database. [IRA medical-provider coordination](https://ira.go.ug/ira-medical-service-providers-meet-to-align-prequalification-requirements/) · [IRA shared-database discussion](https://ira.go.ug/insurance-industry-mull-introduction-of-shared-data-base-to-curb-fraud).

Prioritize explainable checks for duplicate/reused documents or images; conflicting identity, policy, vehicle, loss, claimant, or beneficiary fields; claim frequency/velocity by verified identity, asset, policy, and incident; loss date before cover inception or very soon after inception; repeated provider/repairer/garage/billing patterns; estimate/invoice and item/amount inconsistencies; possible staged/previous damage where reliable inspection evidence exists; and health service/billing anomalies. Each signal needs provenance, confidence/data quality, a reason to investigate, investigator disposition/false-positive feedback, and role-restricted visibility. A shared phone, high claim count, missing documents, or large insured value can have ordinary explanations. Never label a person fraudulent, deny, delay, price, or report them based on a model/rule signal alone. IRA's 2025 discussion of disputes cites undue denials, policy ambiguity, exclusions, valuation disagreements, and claim delays; false positives therefore directly harm genuine claimants. [IRA dispute and claims-handling discussion](https://ira.go.ug/use-alternative-dispute-resolution-mechanisms-justice-ocaya-to-insurers/).

The example “four claims in three days” should be an insurer-configurable, human-reviewed anomaly trigger only after identity resolution, duplicate-event handling, product-specific baselines, and a measurable false-positive review. Do not hard-code it as a fraud threshold. Keep the existing configurable velocity rule as an advisory and evaluate it on historical, de-identified insurer cases before changing defaults.

### Evidence pack acceptance target

Keep the current permissioned, audited JSON export as the API baseline, then make a useful, versioned claim evidence bundle with: claim/policy identifiers and current workflow status; coverage/wording references (with version and relevant source citations); document manifest with original file references/hashes, type, source, received date, extraction fields and confidence; human corrections and missing-evidence requests with reasons; chronology of claim, customer communication, review, assignments, and approvals; human decisions and reasons; export snapshot time, actor, tenant scope, export reason, correlation ID, and integrity manifest. Include only the viewer's authorized content. Keep internal reserves, fraud/risk notes, prompts, restricted legal notes, secrets, and unrelated customer records out of customer-facing bundles. Support a staff/compliance pack and a separately redacted customer disclosure pack only after audience and privacy policy are explicit. Test large files, empty/partial claims, retries, access expiry, revocation, integrity verification, storage not configured, and tenant/case mismatch. Evidence pack export is a snapshot and audit artifact, not a decision.

Offer a readable PDF summary and, where authorized, a ZIP containing evidence plus the JSON manifest. External reviewers must be able to understand the chronology and find the cited evidence without reconstructing the case manually. Validate this with claims officers and supervisors. Measure pack preparation time, missing-evidence rework and returned packs against their current process; enterprise value remains a hypothesis until the pilot demonstrates it.

### Extraction quality and policy setup acceptance

`AzureDocumentIntelligenceProvider` records fields, confidence and bounding boxes, but its classification can fall back to filename keywords, and classification confidence falls back to `0.70` when provider confidence is missing. Test neutral filenames and misleading names, and label heuristic/unknown confidence honestly. These behaviors cannot establish extraction accuracy or document authenticity.

Use the generated packs for development, then a separate held-out set of public blank forms filled with synthetic data and insurer-authorized, de-identified representative scans. Have an independent human validate labels, field values and source locations; Claude-generated ground truth and back-translation alone are insufficient. Measure document classification precision/recall, critical-field exact match, invoice line/total accuracy, missing-evidence precision/recall, human correction rate, and processing latency by product, document type, scan quality and language. Missing or unreadable values must stay unknown. Agree sample sizes and numeric acceptance targets with the insurer before pilot evaluation; report denominators and failures, not a single overall accuracy score.

For policy onboarding, implement upload -> extract draft clauses/checklist/stages -> review source citations and conflicts -> named insurer approval -> publish version -> pin to product and new claims. Retain the prior version for existing claims, define any explicit migration, and invalidate stale retrieval/evaluator caches. This extends the existing product document matrix and approved knowledge workflow.

### Cross-channel continuity, connectors, and notifications

Cross-channel continuity must be based on a verified customer/account identity, never matching display name, phone text, or social handle alone. Add an auditable `ChannelIdentity` link flow with verification method, actor, time, consent purpose, and unlink/revoke. Keep source conversations and consent channel-scoped; make authorized context available to the assistant only after identity linking and permission checks. When no safe match exists, keep conversations separate and ask staff to reconcile.

**Implementation snapshot (2026-11-02):** Notification routing now has a first working slice: an administrator can configure per-event additional in-app recipients and a deployment-configured Teams workspace channel from Settings or Integrations. The original assigned recipient continues to get the existing in-app notification. Teams remains workspace-wide and is shown as `Not configured` unless the deployment has the live HTTPS webhook configured. Email and WhatsApp staff destinations are not offered as active choices because there is not yet a verified internal-recipient and consent flow. The broader target remains routing by event and person across multiple verified destinations, with delivery status, retry/backoff, quiet hours, escalation, and test-notification controls. Keep routing tenant-scoped and server-resolved. Route minimal summaries and deep links; never send claim documents, PII, reserves, fraud signals, prompts, or restricted notes into a notification. Do not assume an RM has WhatsApp or Teams until the destination is registered and verified.

After linking identities, resolve an authorized customer timeline across channels with message/source references and a freshness-bound summary.
