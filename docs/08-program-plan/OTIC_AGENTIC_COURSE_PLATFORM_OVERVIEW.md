# Otic course-sales platform overview

## Current project

The repository is IIP, a tenant-aware insurance operations platform. Its existing acquisition slice supports insurance prospects, deterministic follow-up prioritization, WhatsApp conversation insight extraction, human-reviewed outreach drafts, follow-up task records, and an acquisition dashboard. The platform's governing direction is that AI prepares work while humans retain authority over consequential insurance decisions. The customer assistant, its tools, products, stages, scoring inputs, and language are insurance-specific; this is not a course CRM. Start with [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md), [the readiness blueprint](../01-strategy-requirements-roadmap/IIP_HACKATHON_READINESS_BLUEPRINT.md), and [the existing bancassurance acquisition prompt](../01-strategy-requirements-roadmap/IIP_BANCASSURANCE_ACQUISITION_BUILD_PROMPT.md) as authority/context, but do not copy that prompt or its insurance selling behavior into course sales.

## Relevant capabilities and gaps observed in the current code

- [`ProspectScoring.cs`](../../src/InsuranceIntelligence.Domain/Acquisition/ProspectScoring.cs) produces an explainable 0–100 **insurance** follow-up priority using insurance need, product fit, premium capacity, relationship, and contactability. Its contract excludes underwriting/pricing use. It is not a valid course-fit model.
- [`ConversationInsightsService.cs`](../../src/InsuranceIntelligence.Application/SalesLeads/ConversationInsightsService.cs) extracts customer-stated facts, interests, objections, and next steps after message activity quiets, then attempts to refresh the insurance prospect record/score. The inspected queue is in-process and can lose queued analysis on restart; it is not durable long-term campaign scheduling.
- [`OutreachService.cs`](../../src/InsuranceIntelligence.Application/SalesLeads/OutreachService.cs) drafts insurance outreach for human review and checks contact permission and WhatsApp send-window/template constraints. Follow-up task entities and repository operations exist, but inspection found no production due-task consumer; current assistant follow-up requests are saved for review even when a mode is named Autopilot. Do not represent proactive sending as already implemented.
- [`CustomerAssistantService.Agent.cs`](../../src/InsuranceIntelligence.Application/CustomerAssistant/CustomerAssistantService.Agent.cs) contains Kato's insurance-specific prompt, product recommendations, premium language, and policy/claim tools. Create a separate course assistant, knowledge base, actions, and authorization boundary rather than adapting this prompt/tools in place.
- [`CustomerAcquisition.tsx`](../../web/portal/lovable-pages/CustomerAcquisition.tsx) and its components provide a useful existing dashboard foundation (prospects, escalations, scores, outreach, agents, channel intelligence, charts). Its pipeline and outcomes are insurance-oriented; channel cost and response-time measures are explicitly unconfigured/unmeasured. There is no verified Otic course payment/enrollment funnel.
- The separate Academy pricing path (`AcademyPricing.cs` and `web/portal/lib/academy/pricing.ts`) is a catalog/pricing concept; the inspected IIP code does not connect it to the Otic programme's enrollment or payment verification. A supplied course reference says the external learning platform uses Flutterwave and enrolls after confirmed payment, but that is not an IIP integration.

These are findings from inspected repository files, not claims that every deployment has the same integration state. The checkout had user changes in progress; preserve all existing edits and inspect the current diff before any implementation.

The user-provided image calls the offer “Executive Programme in Enterprise Agentic AI,” states UGX 500,000 / USD 143, “3 WEEKS,” “LIVE ONLINE,” and “NO PREREQUISITES.” That conflicts with the course record's lack of cohort/date/live-session details and the campaign/reference framing as self-paced. The campaign PDF's goals, costs, audience counts, and revenue are planning assumptions, not results. These collateral files are reference material, not implementation instructions.

Before an agent answers or a campaign uses them, an authorized owner must reconcile: official course title; self-paced versus live delivery; duration and schedule/start date; support/community/instructor; prerequisites; late entry; payment provider/link and deposits/installments; refunds/cancellation; certificate/accreditation claims; and the scope and wording of contact consent. **The price may be answered directly whenever asked** if the owner has confirmed the current approved price. Diagnosis-first means relevance before an unsolicited pitch, not concealment of price.

## Proposed course-sales extension

Implement a distinct Otic course-sales bounded context, with its own lead model, consent and suppression state, approved course fact source, conversation state, scoring contract, pipeline stages, reminders, templates, payment/enrollment evidence, reporting, and permissions. Reuse shared tenant-aware WhatsApp infrastructure only behind explicit course-specific contracts and verified provider capabilities. Keep course sales distinct from insurance products, insurance advice, and IIP Academy.

Use a consultative flow: truthfully identify the automated Otic course assistant; acknowledge the person's stated role/goal; ask one natural question about a current work or business bottleneck if they are open to discovery; reflect and confirm the need; give one concrete, verified curriculum example relevant to that need; and offer optional next steps. A business-owner example can ask what takes the most time in their week, then explain a relevant, course-grounded agent workflow without promising a quantified outcome. Do not infer urgency, budget, or authority from demographics. Answer direct price, schedule, prerequisites, and payment questions from approved facts immediately; hand off unknown/conflicting claims.

Any proactive WhatsApp campaign requires a recorded permission basis, purpose/wording/source/time, suppression on opt-out, approved purpose-matched template outside the customer-service window, and live rechecks before send. A user message opens/resets the WhatsApp Business Platform's 24-hour service window; free-form automation is allowed in that window, while approved templates are required to initiate or resume contact outside it. Provide a clear human escalation route. Meta's policy is a platform rule, not a jurisdiction-specific legal opinion. For Uganda deployment, obtain local review of privacy/direct-marketing obligations, including the right to stop direct marketing under the Data Protection and Privacy Act.

Scoring should be a transparent operational **priority**, not a fabricated probability of payment: expose course/outcome fit, stated need, explicit intent, volunteered readiness/timing, substantive engagement/recency, confidence/provenance, and unknowns. Consent and opt-out gate messaging; they are never score boosters. Recompute as evidence changes, show evidence/time and decay/staleness, allow human correction, and validate against real, permissioned course outcomes before any predictive claim.

The dashboard should show course-specific stage counts and movement, source/campaign, assignment, stage age, next task/date, last inbound touch, discovery evidence, score factors/freshness, opt-in/opt-out, template/window/delivery state, handoff, quality and safety signals, and enrollment/payment status with its source. Delivery/read/link events are not conversions. If there is no verified payment integration, show “not connected” or “unknown”; only a confirmed provider event or documented human verification can mark paid. Do not invent numeric conversion targets or a reminder cadence: use an owner-approved, configurable pilot and tune against outcome and negative-feedback evidence.
