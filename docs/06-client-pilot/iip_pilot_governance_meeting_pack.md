This document is part of the Insurance Intelligence Platform (IIP) documentation set. IIP is an Azure-native, human-reviewed insurance operations platform for insurers, brokers, TPAs, HMOs, bancassurance partners, and microinsurance providers. The current MVP direction is motor claims and document intelligence, with WhatsApp-first customer interaction feeding internal insurer workspaces for claims, channels, customers, documents, approvals, audit, and reporting. AI assists with classification, extraction, summarization, triage, routing, drafting, and evidence preparation. Humans retain authority for regulated and consequential decisions such as claim approval, rejection, settlement, fraud determination, payment release, policy binding, and final underwriting decisions.


## 1. Governance Purpose

The governance meeting pack provides a consistent structure for pilot steering meetings. It ensures decisions, risks, issues, metrics, and dependencies are reviewed regularly and documented.

## 2. Recommended Meeting Cadence

| Meeting | Audience | Frequency | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pilot Steering Meeting | Sponsor, operational owner, Otic delivery, IT/security, claims lead | Weekly | Review progress, risks, decisions, KPIs, and blockers |
| Operational Check-in | Claims lead, users, Otic delivery/support | Daily during go-live week; then as agreed | Resolve workflow issues quickly |
| Technical Check-in | Client IT/security, Otic engineering | As needed | Resolve integration, access, environment, or security issues |
| Closeout Review | Leadership, sponsor, Otic executive/product | End of pilot | Review outcomes and next-phase decision |
