# Build Otic course sales around trust

The current repository is an insurance operations platform with insurance-specific lead scoring, conversation insights, reviewed outreach, and a prospect dashboard—not an Otic course-sales CRM. The useful path is a separate, tenant-safe course-sales bounded context that reuses only verified shared infrastructure, asks focused discovery questions, offers course-grounded examples, and answers direct price questions without delay. Long-term follow-up should require recorded permission, durable scheduling, WhatsApp template/window checks, and immediate suppression on opt-out; a lead score must be an explainable priority aid, not a made-up chance of payment. The course material supports a compelling practical learning story, but the supplied campaign/script/artwork conflict on live delivery, dates, support, and payment terms. Product owners must resolve those claims before an agent uses them. The dashboard should track the complete observable pipeline and verified payment evidence, while explicitly showing unknowns where integration is absent.

Customer-requested reminders are saved for review; no live, durable autonomous scheduler was verified. The existing assistant, Kato, is insurance-specific and can recommend policies; it must not be reused as the course sales agent. ## Adaptive discovery works better as a short conversation than a scripted interrogation

Sales research supports adapting a presentation to the buyer's expressed situation and identifying, listening to, and confirming requirements before presenting relevant benefits. A meta-analysis across 155 samples and more than 31,000 salespeople associated adaptive selling with several performance measures, while a cross-industry study found customer orientation had an inverted-U relationship with performance: useful discovery should be focused rather than endless ([Franke & Park, 2006](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1509/jmkr.43.4.693); [Homburg, Müller & Klarmann, 2011](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1509/jm.75.2.55)). SPIN is a practitioner framework for situation, problem, implication, and need-payoff questions; it informs a flexible design, but does not prove a particular WhatsApp script increases course enrollment ([Huthwaite International, SPIN](https://www.huthwaiteinternational.com/spin-methodology)).

For example, if a person mentions running a business, the assistant can ask, “What takes the most time in your business each week?” It should listen, ask one useful clarifying question, reflect the answer for confirmation, then offer one curriculum-grounded example of how a learner could design and test a copilot or workflow relevant to that task. It should not claim that the course will automate the person's real operation or save a quantified number of hours. If the person asks “How much does it cost?” first, the assistant should answer from the current approved fact source before offering more discovery. A concise, truthful virtual-assistant identity and direct human handoff are sensible product choices; evidence about disclosure effects is context-dependent, so the agent should not disguise itself as a human ([Luo et al., 2019](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mksc.2018.1125); [Dai et al., 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jretconser.2025.104621)).

## Permission and WhatsApp rules must gate every proactive send

Meta's current policy requires the business to have the person's number and opt-in permission for subsequent contact, honor requests to stop, and avoid deceptive, surprising, or spammy messages. On the WhatsApp Business Platform, approved templates are required to initiate a conversation; free-form replies are permitted in the 24-hour customer-service window opened/reset by a user's message, and an approved template is required outside it. Meta allows automation in-window but requires a clear human escalation path. These are Meta platform requirements, not local legal advice ([WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/)).

For Uganda, the Data Protection and Privacy Act gives a data subject the right to require a controller to stop processing data for direct marketing. A local legal review should determine the complete consent, privacy, retention, and electronic-marketing obligations; this research is not a legal opinion ([Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019, §26](https://ict.go.ug/ims/public/site/documents/Data-Protection-and-Privacy-Act-2019.pdf)). The system should preserve purpose-specific consent provenance and suppress immediately after any clear opt-out. A legacy contact list, sent template, read receipt, or one reply is not indefinite permission. Business-initiated sales/payment check-ins should be treated as marketing unless Meta approves another classification for the exact template; do not relabel sales reminders as utility.

Meta recommends timely, relevant messages, monitoring volume and feedback, and cooling down people who stop engaging; it does not prescribe the best reminder interval for this course. There is no source-backed universal cadence, number of attempts, conversion benchmark, or expected uplift here. Begin with an owner-approved configurable pilot, favor reminders the person requested or agreed to, cap attempts, and stop or cool down on non-engagement. Re-check consent, opt-out, local time, template approval/purpose, current window, and task state at dispatch. Use the official Platform API or verified provider; do not automate personal accounts or use unofficial bulk clients ([Meta marketing-message best practices](https://whatsappbusiness.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Best-Practices-for-Marketing-Messages-on-WhatsApp-.pdf); [WhatsApp Messaging Guidelines](https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/messaging-guidelines?lang=en_US)).

## Transparent scores and a full pipeline make follow-up useful and measurable

Vendor models such as Microsoft's Fit, Intent, Recency, Engagement framework and Salesforce's separate fit/intent/engagement rules show how a score can expose its dimensions, but vendor cutoffs are design choices rather than causal evidence for this course. A review of 44 lead-scoring studies found varied scoring approaches but relatively few studies examining their effect on sales performance; the course has no training dataset or validated weights ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/sales/sales-qualification-agent-concepts); [Salesforce scoring rules](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=pc_setup_scoring_rules.htm&language=en_US&type=5); [Järvinen & Taiminen review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9890437/)). Start with an explainable priority summary: course/outcome fit, buyer-stated need, explicit intent, self-reported timing, substantive engagement/recency, and evidence confidence/freshness. Show evidence and unknowns. Consent gates messaging but does not boost score. Until sufficient real course outcomes are available, do not display a probability of payment.

Define course stages by observable evidence: new/permission status, engaged, discovery, need confirmed, course details shared, considering/question pending, enrollment intent, registration/payment instructions, verified enrollment, and explicit terminal states. The dashboard should show stage counts and age, source/campaign, owner, next agreed action, last inbound, discovery evidence, score dimensions and freshness, consent and template eligibility, handoff, quality indicators, and payment/enrollment source. Report funnel denominators and cohort windows. Template delivery, open/read status, or a checkout link is not a sale; paid status requires a provider event or audited human verification. CRM-vendor sources recommend stage conversion, time-in-stage, and stalled-opportunity monitoring, but those are operating practices rather than guaranteed improvements ([Salesforce Sales Stage Analysis](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=sales.Stage_analysis_overview.htm&language=en_US&type=5); [Close pipeline design](https://help.close.com/best-practices/build-a-sales-process/designing-your-pipeline)).

## Conclusion

The right change is a bounded course-sales product that earns the next conversation through relevance and gives people control over timing, facts, and handoff. The codebase offers reusable patterns, but its insurance assumptions and incomplete follow-up/payment paths make separation and honest capability reporting essential. Start by resolving offer truth and contact permission, then build observable workflow foundations, human-reviewed messaging, and only later durable proactive sends and verified enrollment reporting.
