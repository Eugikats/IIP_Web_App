# Platform and Otic Campaign Materials

## What does the current platform already do for acquisition, conversations, scoring, follow-up, and reporting?

### Takeaway
IIP already contains an insurance-specific lead-management, conversation-insight, score-prioritization, WhatsApp-draft, and acquisition-dashboard path. It is not an Otic course CRM: the assistant, score features, stages, products, actions, and purchase outcomes are tied to insurance. Persistent customer-requested follow-up records exist, but the inspected path saves them for review; it is not a course-sales autonomous follow-up scheduler.

### Cited Findings
- IIP's root instructions define it as a tenant-isolated insurance operations platform and state that AI prepares work while humans make consequential insurance decisions; its docs explicitly treat customer-facing insurance assistant behavior as informational/routing and caution against unlicensed product recommendations. [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md); [IIP_HACKATHON_READINESS_BLUEPRINT.md](../../docs/01-strategy-requirements-roadmap/IIP_HACKATHON_READINESS_BLUEPRINT.md); [IIP_BANCASSURANCE_ACQUISITION_BUILD_PROMPT.md](../../docs/01-strategy-requirements-roadmap/IIP_BANCASSURANCE_ACQUISITION_BUILD_PROMPT.md)
- The existing `ProspectScoring` domain score is deterministic and 0–100. Its five components are insurance need (35), insurance product fit (25), capacity to pay an insurance premium (15), bank/insurer relationship (15), and contactability (10). Its own contract says it prioritizes follow-up and is not an underwriting or pricing signal. It selects up to two insurance product fits and carries reason strings and missing facts. [ProspectScoring.cs](../../src/InsuranceIntelligence.Domain/Acquisition/ProspectScoring.cs)
- The insurance prospect record and stages are domain-specific (`New`, `GettingToKnow`, `Interested`, `Considering`, `ReadyToApply`, `Applied`, `WithAPerson`, `NotNow`); the dashboard labels and derives those same states. A course funnel needs different stage meanings and separate conversion events. [Prospects.cs](../../src/InsuranceIntelligence.Domain/Acquisition/Prospects.cs); [CustomerAcquisition.tsx](../../web/portal/lovable-pages/CustomerAcquisition.tsx)
- Conversation insight processing rereads a WhatsApp thread after a 20-second quiet period, extracts customer-stated facts/interests/objections/next steps, and attempts to rescore a lead. The inspected queue is in-process and bounded (500 pending); a restart can lose queued analysis work, and scoring can fail without losing the saved conversation insight. This is message-burst analysis, not a durable multi-day outreach scheduler. [ConversationInsightsService.cs](../../src/InsuranceIntelligence.Application/SalesLeads/ConversationInsightsService.cs); [ConversationInsightsQueue.cs](../../src/InsuranceIntelligence.Api/Agents/ConversationInsightsQueue.cs)
- `OutreachService` drafts insurance introductions and quiet-client / policy-ending follow-ups. A quiet follow-up is drafted after three days for an interested lead with agreed contact, a phone number, and no application/human handoff. Drafts are explicitly for review; sending additionally checks consent, local sending hours, and WhatsApp's open 24-hour customer-service window or an approved template. [OutreachService.cs](../../src/InsuranceIntelligence.Application/SalesLeads/OutreachService.cs)

The assistant tool accepts a customer-requested date/time and writes `PendingReview`, even when the saved mode says `Autopilot`; its response says it will not send automatically. Messaging-mode API exposes `AutopilotAvailable` based on deployment configuration, while current endpoint tests expect false. Treat follow-up sending as unavailable until an actual worker, gate, provider-template validation, and live status are verified. Preserve these user changes; build against the current checkout and do not overwrite or reset them. This is an observed repository-state fact, not a committed source document.

### Inferences
- Add a course-sales bounded context rather than adapting the insurance score, assistant prompt, insurance stages, or insurance conversion definitions. It can reuse tenant-aware messaging infrastructure only behind explicit course-specific contracts and authorization.
- Treat lead score as a transparent prioritization aid, not as a claimed probability that a person will pay. Candidate dimensions are evidenced course/problem fit, explicit intent, buying readiness/asked next step, response/engagement recency, and evidence confidence/data completeness. Consent and opt-out should gate whether contact is allowed, not increase persuasiveness or override a refusal.
- The requested “diagnose before price” sequence fits a permission-based consultative flow: ask one contextual question about work/role and current bottleneck; reflect the answer; offer one verified course example relevant to that bottleneck; explain fit and limits; then provide program/price/link if invited or directly asked. Never withhold a requested price as a pressure tactic.
- The dashboard should expose real course stages, last customer touch, next action/date, contact permission/source, conversation-sourced needs with evidence references, score factors and freshness, course-link/payment events, handoff, opt-out, and campaign/channel attribution. Payment conversion must be based on confirmed provider or platform events, not agent inference or a click.

### Gaps
- The source repo's insurance prospect score has no empirical course enrollment dataset, course-fit labels, calibration evidence, or defensible weights; a future course score needs validation against actual outcomes and should show its evidence and uncertainty.
- Follow-up records have due-claim support but no verified delivery worker. Availability, retries/idempotency, template attestation, cancellation on reply/opt-out/payment, quiet hours, and recovery need concrete implementation evidence before proactive sending is called live.
- No inspected IIP integration proves that Otic course payments, enrollment, refunds, cohorts, or applications can be reconciled. Course payment remains a separate-system claim in the supplied reference until confirmed by a real integration.

## What should the improvement build prompt require for an ethical, effective Otic course-sales journey and richer sales dashboard?

### Takeaway
Specify a distinct Otic course-sales workflow that uses conversation context to discover the prospect's role and work bottleneck before explaining relevant course value, then supports transparent next steps and consent-safe follow-up. The build prompt should insist on explicit permission, honest identity, easy human handoff/opt-out, durable scheduled tasks, evidence-backed analytics, and no pressure or invented course facts.

### Cited Findings
- Existing code already demonstrates that conversation-derived lead facts and interests can be updated after a thread quiets, that scores can show readable evidence/reasons and missing information, and that outreach can be blocked absent consent or outside WhatsApp's allowed service window unless a valid template is used. These are useful patterns to adapt only after replacing insurance semantics with course semantics. [ConversationInsightsService.cs](../../src/InsuranceIntelligence.Application/SalesLeads/ConversationInsightsService.cs); [ProspectScoring.cs](../../src/InsuranceIntelligence.Domain/Acquisition/ProspectScoring.cs); [OutreachService.cs](../../src/InsuranceIntelligence.Application/SalesLeads/OutreachService.cs)
- The current assistant has clear interaction patterns that transfer: short replies, one or two relevant questions at most, use details the person already gave, do not repeat discovery questions, skip questions when upset/in a hurry/asking for a human, and respect “no/not now.” Its insurance product pitch/recommendation/tool rules do not transfer. [CustomerAssistantService.Agent.cs](../../src/InsuranceIntelligence.Application/CustomerAssistant/CustomerAssistantService.Agent.cs)
- The current repo requires tenant/workspace permission resolution server-side, audited sensitive actions and communication, untrusted customer/model content, no fabricated data or fake sends/metrics, and user-visible UI compliant with `DESIGN.md`. Applicable docs also require a readiness blueprint/work order and note that no live WhatsApp readiness may be inferred from unit tests. [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md); [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md); [IIP_HACKATHON_READINESS_BLUEPRINT.md](../../docs/01-strategy-requirements-roadmap/IIP_HACKATHON_READINESS_BLUEPRINT.md)
- Course conversion should use real, distinct outcome events such as outreach delivered, reply, discovery completed, course details requested/sent, application/link initiated, checkout started, payment confirmed, enrolled, not now, opted out, and human handoff. The current insurance report's `Applied`/“conversion” semantics are not enrollment/payment and should not be reused as course revenue. [AcquisitionReports.cs](../../src/InsuranceIntelligence.Application/SalesLeads/AcquisitionReports.cs); [ChannelIntelligenceTab.tsx](../../web/portal/components/acquisition/tabs/ChannelIntelligenceTab.tsx)

### Inferences
- A course consultation can follow: (1) disclose that the person is interacting with Otic's automated assistant where applicable; (2) identify what kind of work/business they do if not known; (3) ask what task or work problem takes the most time or causes friction; (4) clarify what they have tried / what outcome matters, without turning the conversation into a qualification form; (5) mirror the need and connect one specific verified module/outcome or an illustrative, clearly hypothetical AI example; (6) ask whether the person wants program details; (7) answer cost, structure, prerequisites, and payment questions directly; (8) offer the approved apply/payment route or a human; (9) schedule only a permissioned follow-up with purpose/time/window and stop when replied, paid, opted out, or handed off.
- For someone who mentions running a business, a natural discovery opener is “What takes the most time in your business day to day?” Then clarify a concrete workflow (for example, repetitive customer questions, drafting, reporting, or organizing information) and use a clearly labeled hypothetical to explain how a course learner could design/test an agent. Do not claim the course ships a production automation for their business or guarantees productivity/revenue.
- Course-sales stages should match real lifecycle behavior, for example: New / permission pending → Engaged → Need diagnosed → Fit discussed → Details requested/shared → Application or checkout started → Payment confirmed/enrolled; plus Not now, Not a fit, Human follow-up, and Opted out. Store events as a history; define explicit, auditable stage transitions and let humans correct mistaken extraction.
- Provide a transparent lead-priority score with component values, source conversation turn/evidence, last-updated time, confidence, and missing fields. Recompute on new conversation, payment or other outcome event, and data staleness; evaluate score usefulness against actual paid enrollment and retention outcomes before presenting it as predictive. Keep permission and suppression status as hard contact gates rather than scoring boosts.
- Dashboard candidates: pipeline counts and conversion by stage/source/campaign; time-to-first-response and time-in-stage; reply/appointment/course-details/application/payment/enrollment rates; confirmed revenue and paid amount only from verified payment events; consent source/time/status, template/sender and opt-out; next best action with owner and due time; follow-up delivery/retry/failure; per-agent performance with denominator and period; score distribution/reasons/confidence; conversation insights with source evidence; stale records; and honest “Not configured/Not measured” values when integration data is absent. Avoid fake trend series, unsupported conversion probability, or vanity measures without definitions.
- Long-term proactive contact should be useful and sparse: base follow-up on an expressed interest, unanswered question, or customer-requested next step; send a brief message tied to what they said; ask one low-friction check-in question (e.g., whether they have any questions or whether they completed payment); provide a clear stop-contacting option; and suppress reminders if they paid, replied, withdrew consent, or requested no contact. Do not infer permission from an imported list, email click, score, or prior unrelated community membership.
- Because course outreach aims to drive tuition payment, implement a payment-state integration/reconciliation path rather than giving the chat agent authority to mark a lead “paid.” Keep payment action and access/enrollment ownership with the actual checkout/course platform and reflect only confirmed provider events in IIP.

### Gaps
- Current UI authority requires reading the four linked design documents in `DESIGN.md` before proposing any user-visible dashboard change; this note only inventories current screens and repository constraints.
- No Meta Business Platform policy or current platform rule was independently researched in this local code/materials task. Consent, template, service-window, business-initiated messaging, and rate assumptions need current official Meta source verification in the broader research.
- No campaign conversion data or course enrollment/payment event samples were provided; score weights, follow-up intervals, dashboard targets, and “best converting” angles cannot be evidence-set yet. Treat defaults as configurable hypotheses and instrument tests before optimizing.
- The course record's live currency/price/payment details, accepted payment routes, and access/enrollment reconciliation have not been independently verified. Do not implement fake payment state or code a merchant code from the PDF placeholders.
