# WhatsApp Policy for Consent-Based Course Sales and Proactive Follow-Up

## What permission, opt-in, and opt-out controls should the course-sales system enforce?

### Takeaway
Before any business-initiated sales or follow-up message, the system should require a recorded, active permission for communications from the named Otic business, disclose the kind of messages a person can expect, and honor withdrawal immediately. Meta's current policy is the controlling platform baseline; it leaves the collection method flexible but does not make possession of a phone number, a prior unrelated transaction, or a template approval equivalent to permission.

### Cited Findings
- Meta's WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy was last updated **September 23, 2026** (checked November 2, 2026). It says a business may contact a person only if the person provided their mobile number or username **and** granted opt-in permission for subsequent messages or calls from the business. The business is responsible for obtaining permission in a manner that complies with applicable law. [WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/)
- The policy requires honoring requests made either on or off WhatsApp to block, discontinue, or opt out, and prohibits confusing, deceptive, misleading, spammy, or surprising communications. [WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/)
- Meta's policy recommends stating the message categories, collecting separate opt-ins by category, separately obtaining permission to initiate calls, providing clear opt-out instructions, and explaining the value of receiving the messages. Meta says it may reactively review opt-in flows and user feedback. [WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy — Best Practices for Opt-In](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/)
- Meta's April 2026 guide says marketing messages should be expected, timely, and relevant; it says businesses should identify themselves and make the message types clear, and recommends separately asking for explicit promotional consent rather than bundling it with transactional updates. It also recommends monitoring quality ratings and giving disengaged recipients a cooldown. [Best Practices for Marketing Messages on WhatsApp (PDF)](https://whatsappbusiness.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Best-Practices-for-Marketing-Messages-on-WhatsApp-.pdf)
- The April 2026 marketing guide contains a footnote saying “WhatsApp specific opt-in consent is no longer required,” while the same document says permission must be obtained in advance and the current Business Messaging Policy still requires opt-in permission. Read the footnote narrowly as not requiring one WhatsApp-exclusive collection mechanism; it is not permission to message without advance permission, clear identity/purpose, or applicable legal permission. The guide's exact wording is ambiguous without its linked explanatory material, so policy and local-law review should govern implementation. [Best Practices for Marketing Messages on WhatsApp (PDF)](https://whatsappbusiness.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Best-Practices-for-Marketing-Messages-on-WhatsApp-.pdf); [WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/)
- Meta's marketing guidance lists possible collection touchpoints such as a website, SMS, customer-service call, IVR, QR code, in-person flow, transaction flow, Messenger, click-to-WhatsApp ad, or WhatsApp thread, while advising transparency about the message types. [Best Practices for Marketing Messages on WhatsApp (PDF)](https://whatsappbusiness.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Best-Practices-for-Marketing-Messages-on-WhatsApp-.pdf)
- Meta's terms/policy prohibit asking users to share or sharing full payment-card numbers, financial account numbers, personal ID card numbers, or other sensitive identifiers in WhatsApp. [WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/)

### Inferences
- Treat a course lead's follow-up about considering/enrolling/paying for the programme as marketing/sales messaging unless Meta approves another classification for the exact template. A receipt or course-access notice may have a different purpose, but do not relabel a sales chase as utility; submit the template with its actual purpose and follow Meta's decision.
- Persist consent evidence per person and per purpose/category: source, timestamp and timezone, wording/disclosure version, named Otic entity, permitted message categories, channel/call permissions where applicable, current status, withdrawal timestamp/source, and the events that produced status changes. This gives the campaign scheduler an auditable basis to exclude unconsented and withdrawn contacts.
- A robust opt-out system should accept natural-language stop requests (and other clear equivalent requests), suppress future campaign and agent sends before queue dispatch, log the change, and offer an easy re-opt-in route. Do not rely on template approval or “STOP” wording alone as the only way to honor an opt-out.
- For launches in Uganda or other countries, map applicable local privacy and electronic-marketing rules separately; Meta makes the business responsible for local-law compliance. This research establishes Meta policy only, not a legal conclusion for any jurisdiction.

### Gaps
- The April 2026 guide's “WhatsApp specific opt-in consent is no longer required” footnote links to explanatory material that was not accessible during this research. Its precise scope is therefore not independently verified. The current policy's general opt-in requirement remains clear.
- No country-specific legal review of consent, marketing communications, privacy notices, or retention rules was conducted. Obtain jurisdiction-specific legal review before launch.

## What controls apply to proactive messages and agent replies on the WhatsApp Business Platform?

### Takeaway
On the WhatsApp Business Platform (including its APIs), an automated agent can reply freely only within the 24-hour customer-service window opened and reset by each user message. Outside that window the business must use an approved message template; proactive lead nurturing and reminders therefore require both valid permission and an approved template suited to the actual message purpose.

### Cited Findings
- Under the current policy, the Platform-specific rule is: initiate conversations only with approved Message Templates and use each template only for its designated purpose; Meta may review, approve, pause, or reject templates. [WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy, §2](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/)
- Within the Platform's 24-hour customer-service window, which opens and resets with each user message, the business may reply without a template. Outside that window, it may only send approved templates. [WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy, §2](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/)
- Meta allows automation during the 24-hour window but requires prompt, clear, direct escalation paths, including in-chat transfer to a human, phone, email, web support, in-person support, or support form. [WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy, §2](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/)
- Meta describes marketing messages as supporting awareness, lead nurturing, sales, offers, assisted selling, and re-engagement after a lapse. [Marketing Messages](https://whatsappbusiness.com/products/conversation-categories/marketing/)
- Meta's Platform features describe business-initiated messages after opt-in as customizable templates, which can be text, media, or interactive; they also describe buttons, rich media, interactive messages, and CRM/backend integrations. [Business Platform Features](https://whatsappbusiness.com/products/business-platform/features/)
- Meta states Platform messages are charged based on message category; as of the FAQ checked November 2, 2026, marketing, utility, and authentication messages are charged per delivered message beginning July 1, 2025, and service messages became chargeable per message on October 1, 2026, subject to the published free allowance described there. [WhatsApp Business FAQ](https://whatsappbusiness.com/resources/faq/)
- Meta says the Platform provides programmatic access and CRM/marketing-stack integrations for businesses messaging at scale, while the WhatsApp Business App is a free-to-download app for smaller businesses with limited customization and integration. [WhatsApp Business FAQ](https://whatsappbusiness.com/resources/faq/)

### Inferences
- Build separate sending paths for (a) agent replies while the live 24-hour window is open and (b) scheduled/proactive sends requiring a current approved template. Re-check window state, consent, suppression, template approval/category, recipient status, and provider sendability at execution time, not only when creating a task.
- Persist inbound-message timestamps and derive window expiration from the latest inbound event; do not assume an outgoing message or a queued job extends the window. If the window expires, block free-form text and require an approved template or wait for the person to message again.
- Make template registry data versioned and explicit (Meta template name/language/category, approval status, designated use, variables, last verification, account/phone-number scope). Keep course enrolment reminders as marketing unless the exact content is properly classified by Meta; transactional updates should remain purpose-specific.
- Keep the course agent transparent about being an automated Otic assistant and offer a clear human handoff, matching Meta's no-impersonation and escalation requirements.
- The app/API distinction is consequential: the requested autonomous, scheduled CRM follow-up requires a supported Platform/API integration and webhook-aware scheduler, not unofficial automation layered over a handset app. If the current number uses the Business App, confirm supported coexistence/migration options with Meta or the provider before designing an API transition.

### Gaps
- Meta developer pages for template API details, error codes, and policy enforcement returned rate-limit/access errors in this research session. Confirm current API-specific send semantics, retry guidance, template status webhooks, and provider behavior against the chosen Meta API/provider docs during implementation.
- Template category review is dynamic and the correct classification depends on the submitted content and current Meta review; no course-specific template was submitted or classified here.

## How should the system protect message quality, platform standing, and accurate measurement?

### Takeaway
Proactive outreach must optimize for recipient expectation and usefulness, not message volume. Monitor delivery, replies, blocks/reports where available, opt-outs, template/account quality signals, and downstream enrollment/payment outcomes; throttle or pause outreach when quality worsens, and never use unofficial bulk or handset automation.

### Cited Findings
- Meta says significant negative feedback, harm, or policy violations can lead it to limit or remove WhatsApp Business access or messaging ability; its systems may limit message volume or calls based on user feedback. It may use automated processing and human review of available account/profile information and reported messages. [WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy, §7](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/)
- Meta's WhatsApp Messaging Guidelines, last updated September 23, 2026, prohibit adversarial behavior including unauthorized bulk messaging, auto-messaging, auto-dialing, or automation used to harm WhatsApp or its users; repeated unwanted contact is described as spam. These guidelines apply alongside the Business Messaging Policy to WhatsApp Business users. [WhatsApp Messaging Guidelines](https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/messaging-guidelines?lang=en_US)
- Meta's April 2026 marketing guide advises businesses to choose a relevant time, monitor recipients' marketing-message volume per day and week, avoid excessive promotions, and cool down recipients whose engagement with templates stops. It says high-quality marketing messages are expected, timely, personalized, relevant, and have clear calls to action. [Best Practices for Marketing Messages on WhatsApp (PDF)](https://whatsappbusiness.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Best-Practices-for-Marketing-Messages-on-WhatsApp-.pdf)
- Meta's Platform onboarding guide warns that eroded personalization, excessive messaging, or violations of opt-in boundaries create risk, and says low quality ratings can result in reduced message limits or account blocks. [Onboarding to the WhatsApp Business Platform (PDF)](https://whatsappbusiness.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Onboarding-to-the-WhatsApp-Business-Platform.pdf)
- Meta's official FAQ says the Business App supports smaller-scale communication from a single device and has limited integrations; the Platform offers programmatic messaging, richer features, integrations, and scale. [WhatsApp Business FAQ](https://whatsappbusiness.com/resources/faq/)
- Meta says commercial sales using WhatsApp commerce features are subject to the Commerce Policy and applicable laws; the business is responsible for sales terms and transaction fulfillment. [WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy, §“Policy for WhatsApp Commerce Features”](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/)

### Inferences
- Engineer campaign guardrails: per-person/channel frequency caps, quiet hours by recipient timezone, maximum attempts per sequence, cooldown after non-engagement, stop on reply/payment/opt-out/complaint, deduplication, campaign-level kill switch, and a queue circuit breaker for increased errors or quality deterioration. Meta does not publish a universal reminder cadence in the sources reviewed; derive initial limits conservatively and tune using measured outcomes and recipient feedback.
- For a course funnel, measure qualified reply, completed application/checkout, verified payment, time to conversion, opt-out, complaint/block proxies, template quality, delivery failures, and cost per paid enrollee. Don't report a conversation or click as a sale; if no verified payment integration exists, show payment as unknown/pending and expose the data source.
- A quality-aware dashboard should distinguish policy/consent eligibility, template approval/delivery state, active service-window status, and CRM sales stage; an agent score cannot override any messaging eligibility gate.
- Implement with the official WhatsApp Business Platform API or an approved integration/provider. Do not automate personal WhatsApp accounts, scrape contact lists, or use unofficial clients for scheduled campaigns.

### Gaps
- Meta's technical API documentation for current template-quality thresholds, recipient-level marketing limits, exact enforcement signals, and webhook fields could not be fetched due to access/rate limiting; the marketing guide supports watching quality and engagement but does not establish numeric thresholds.
- No universally valid send cadence, cooldown duration, or conversion-uplift benchmark was located in current primary Meta sources. These should be treated as configurable hypotheses and evaluated in a consented pilot.
- This research covers Meta platform rules and product documentation. It does not establish compliance with local privacy, direct-marketing, telecom, consumer-protection, education, or payment laws.
