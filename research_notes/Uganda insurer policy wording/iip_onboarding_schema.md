# IIP policy wording onboarding: code-fit notes

## Takeaway

The onboarding flow is designed to turn a stored policy wording into **reviewable claim-process proposals**, not to build or approve a complete insurance product from the policy. The extractor proposes claim types, claim-required documents, claim questions, covered items, and exclusions; every proposal needs a source quote, and staff accept/edit/reject proposals before accepted items are added to a new draft product version. Product publication remains subject to its ordinary approval gate. See `docs/build-loop/BUILD_PLAN.md:85-92`, `src/InsuranceIntelligence.Application/Products/WordingExtraction.cs:42-55`, and `src/InsuranceIntelligence.Domain/Products/WordingDraft.cs:28-37,67-72`.

For onboarding test material, generate a **clearly synthetic, Uganda-oriented private motor comprehensive policy wording** with numbered clauses, explicit claim categories, a concise “how to claim” section, document requirements tied to claim categories, customer questions, and separate cover/exclusion clauses. Include page markers and keep it below 60,000 characters. The repository's existing test wording is a useful format reference: `web/portal/tests/fixtures/claims-core/sample_policy_wording.txt:1-29`.

## Current extraction targets

- **Covered items and exclusions:** title/detail plus exact source quote and clause; stored as product `CoverageItems` with kind, clause, page, and quote. These are informational grounding for staff and the assistant and do not decide a claim (`src/InsuranceIntelligence.Application/Products/WordingExtraction.cs:50-51`; `src/InsuranceIntelligence.Domain/Products/Product.cs:49-52,101-109`).
- **Claim types:** title/detail, source quote, clause, and optional aliases. These become the product's claim type definitions (`WordingExtraction.cs:52`; `src/InsuranceIntelligence.Domain/Products/Product.cs:38-42,111-120`).
- **Claim documents:** customer-facing title, reason/detail, source quote/clause, one `documentKey`, applicable claim type names, and required flag (`WordingExtraction.cs:53`). The key must exactly match (case-insensitively) a built-in or active workspace document-type key. Unknown or empty keys make the item non-acceptable until a real key is chosen (`WordingExtraction.cs:150-164`; `src/InsuranceIntelligence.Application/Products/WordingDraftService.cs:340-354`). Default keys include `ClaimForm`, `PoliceAbstract`, `Invoice`, `MedicalReport`, `PhotoEvidence`, `CustomerId`, and `GarageEstimate` (`src/InsuranceIntelligence.Domain/Documents/DocumentTypeDefinition.cs:72-86`).
- **Claim questions/fields:** question title, help text, exact quote/clause, field type, optional select options, claim types, and required flag (`WordingExtraction.cs:54`). Supported field values in the application parser include `Text`, `Number`, `Date`, `Select`, `Phone`, `Currency`, and `YesNo`; malformed types fall back to `Text`, and `Select` without at least two options becomes `Text` (`WordingExtraction.cs:169-181`).
- **Source citations:** quote verification strips punctuation/spacing and checks that at least 12 letters/digits match; a verified quote can get a clause inferred from preceding numbering and a page inferred from form-feed boundaries or a `Page N` marker (`WordingExtraction.cs:33-37,217-255`). The wording is read from stored knowledge chunks in order and cut to 60,000 characters; truncation is recorded (`src/InsuranceIntelligence.Application/Products/WordingDraftService.cs:45-54`; `WordingExtraction.cs:33-36,63-68`).
- **Review and application:** wording is uploaded to Knowledge as type `PolicyWording`, selected on a product page, and read there (`docs/build-loop/HOW_TO_TEST.md:47-52`; `web/portal/components/iip/WordingDraftPanel.tsx:65-88`). Each proposal is reviewed; applying accepted items is additive, creates a new draft version, and does not publish it (`docs/build-loop/BUILD_PLAN.md:86-92`; `src/InsuranceIntelligence.Application/Products/WordingDraftService.cs:138-182`).

## Test document recommendations

For the principal onboarding test, generate one short policy wording with:

1. A fictional insurer and product, an explicit “synthetic test document” label, version/effective-date placeholders, and a table of contents or clearly numbered sections. Use consistent clause numbering (`1.1`, `2.1`, `3.1`) and explicit `Page 1`, `Page 2`, etc. lines; those give the citation parser the markers it recognizes.
2. Separate cover clauses for at least **accidental damage, theft, fire, third-party property damage, and third-party bodily injury**, then a separate exclusions section. Give each clause a distinctive sentence long enough to quote exactly.
3. A claims section with distinct categories such as own damage, theft, windscreen, and third-party injury/property damage. Tie each requested item to the relevant category and say whether it is required or conditional.
4. Document names that map to the current workspace's available keys: e.g. claim form, police abstract/report, invoice/repair invoice, medical report, damage photos, customer ID, and garage estimate. If testing a logbook, driving licence, proof of ownership, keys, or assessor report, first create an active custom document type for each; the parser only accepts keys already in the workspace catalog. Custom keys may be tracked as requirements, but the document-type code notes custom types are not automatically recognized until they have real examples (`src/InsuranceIntelligence.Domain/Documents/DocumentTypeDefinition.cs:10-17,31-52`).
5. A short “information to provide” section with clear questions and answers of realistic data types (incident date/time, location, vehicle registration, driver name, whether injury occurred, police reference). Avoid enumerated-choice questions for the first run unless testing the `Select` UI mismatch below.
6. Exact clause text in selectable/native text, no image-only scans for this onboarding test. Keep it safely below 60,000 characters so the required-doc and claim-question sections are not truncated.

The existing synthetic fixture already demonstrates headings, page markers, differentiated motor cover/exclusion clauses, per-claim document asks, and a final information-questions section (`web/portal/tests/fixtures/claims-core/sample_policy_wording.txt:1-29`).

## Gaps / cautions

- **This is not whole-policy setup.** The extraction schema does not create product identity/class, IRA approval reference/effective date, premium/rating/limits/excess, eligibility/underwriting rules, lifecycle stages, policy schedule, or legal contract terms. Those are separate product configuration or source-document concerns (`src/InsuranceIntelligence.Domain/Products/Product.cs:23-47,82-92`; `WordingExtraction.cs:49-55`). In the generation prompt, don't claim the generated document has onboarded all product settings.
- **Document-key dependency:** document requirements are only usable when their key exists among built-ins or active workspace catalog entries. The existing manual test explicitly expects the log book proposal to be blocked until a document type is chosen (`docs/build-loop/HOW_TO_TEST.md:48-51`). Preconfigure custom motor evidence keys before testing them.
- **UI field-type mismatch:** the application prompt/parser permits `Select`, but `WordingDraftPanel.tsx`'s `FIELD_TYPES` list omits `Select` (`src/InsuranceIntelligence.Application/Products/WordingExtraction.cs:54,169-181`; `web/portal/components/iip/WordingDraftPanel.tsx:32`). Use simple text/date/number/yes-no questions in the generated wording for a clean onboarding run; treat select questions as a separate UI gap to fix/test.
- **Citations are textual, not legal interpretation.** Quotes must reproduce wording exactly; parser accepts punctuation/spacing differences but needs a 12-character alphanumeric match (`WordingExtraction.cs:37,217-227`). A supplied clause label may be retained even when it is not derived from the text, so the synthetic prompt should make clause labels accurate and generated text extractable. It does not implement OCR correction or prove a clause's legal meaning.
- **Page citations depend on extraction markers.** PDF/Word ingestion may not preserve visible pagination as `Page N` or form feeds. For reliable fixture results, include explicit page-marker lines and inspect the extracted text after upload (`WordingExtraction.cs:230-255`).
- **Keep it synthetic and non-authoritative.** Use invented insurer/product names and mark the wording as a test artifact. Do not pass generated sample terms off as a real Ugandan insurer's approved wording; no legal or regulatory sufficiency is established by this code review.
