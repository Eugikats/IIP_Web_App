# Uganda insurer product mix and policy-wording test priorities

## What does an “average Ugandan insurer” product mix look like in available market evidence?

### Takeaway

There is no single public “average insurer” catalog established by the sources reviewed. The latest IRA annual report, for 2025, shows total life and non-life gross written premiums at UGX 978.85bn and UGX 1,001.54bn respectively; the latest UIA product-class ranking located is for 2024, when individual life, medical, motor comprehensive, fire, deposit-administered schemes and credit life were the six leading classes. These figures indicate market importance, not the share of insurers offering each class or a typical menu for every insurer. ([IRA 2025 Annual Insurance Market Report listing](https://ira.go.ug/download/2025-insurance-industry-annual-market-report-2/), [UIA 2024 Integrated Report](https://uia.co.ug/sites/default/files/2025-06/UIA%20Annual%20Report%202024.pdf))

### Cited Findings

- IRA’s 2025 annual report gives life gross written premium of UGX 978.85bn and non-life gross written premium of UGX 1,001.54bn, nearly balanced segment totals; IRA lists the report as uploaded October 8, 2026. ([IRA 2025 Annual Insurance Market Report](https://ira.go.ug/download/2025-insurance-industry-annual-market-report-2/))
- UIA’s 2024 report lists these top six classes by gross written premium: Individual Life (UGX 379.22bn), Medical (UGX 354.92bn), Motor Comprehensive (UGX 191.23bn), Fire (UGX 174.05bn), Deposit Administered Schemes (UGX 98.26bn), and Credit Life (UGX 81.87bn). The report labels the figures as unaudited industry statistics and cites IRA Q4 2024 statistics. ([UIA 2024 Integrated Report, table 03](https://uia.co.ug/sites/default/files/2025-06/UIA%20Annual%20Report%202024.pdf))
- In the same report, the top three classes by gross claims incurred are Individual Life (UGX 188.79bn), Medical (UGX 263.17bn), and Motor Comprehensive (UGX 88bn). ([UIA 2024 Integrated Report, table 04](https://uia.co.ug/sites/default/files/2025-06/UIA%20Annual%20Report%202024.pdf))
- IRA’s 2024 annual market report separately classifies non-life revenue across motor, health and medical, fire, personal accident, engineering, marine, liability, theft, workers’ compensation, agriculture, bonds, aviation, energy and miscellaneous; it reports 2024 insurance revenue of UGX 215.99bn for health and medical, UGX 190.28bn for fire, UGX 189.67bn for motor and UGX 115.41bn for personal accident. ([IRA 2024 Annual Insurance Market Report](https://ira.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2024-Insurance-Industry-market-report.pdf))
- IRA’s 2024 life-company table uses classes including life assurances, group life, group credit, health insurance, annuities and deposit administration; 2024 reported revenue was UGX 131.72bn for life assurances, UGX 39.24bn for group life, UGX 82.92bn for group credit, UGX 124.54bn for health insurance, and UGX 3.31bn for deposit administration. ([IRA 2024 Annual Insurance Market Report](https://ira.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2024-Insurance-Industry-market-report.pdf))
- The Insurance Act recognizes life insurance and non-life classes including fire, burglary, personal accident, employers’ and public liability, marine hull and cargo, aviation, motor, crops/fishing/livestock, bonds, contractors’ all risks, machinery breakdown/installation/boiler explosion, health insurance and health membership. ([Insurance Act, Cap. 213](https://www.businesslicences.go.ug/uploads/documents/theinsuranceact-1.pdf))
- The IRA FAQ describes motor third-party, accident-fire-and-theft, and comprehensive as distinct motor cover types; the IRA describes comprehensive as combining third-party bodily injury/property liability and own-vehicle accidental damage, fire or theft. ([IRA Frequently Asked Questions](https://ira.go.ug/frequently-asked-questions/))
- UIA’s non-life FAQs describe motor third-party as a policy for vehicle owners covering third-party bodily injury or death; the association’s non-life overview also describes property, motor, health, marine, travel, liability, professional indemnity and burglary insurance. ([UIA Non-Life FAQs](https://www.uia.co.ug/non-life-faqs), [UIA Non-Life Insurance](https://uia.co.ug/page/non-life-insurance))
- Official product catalogs illustrate that composite insurers can span many classes: GA lists fire/property, bonds, engineering, liability, motor, accident, medical, marine and aviation products; ICEA LION Uganda presents personal accident, medical, life/family, motor and home/property products; AAR Uganda lists motor, fire, burglary, electronic equipment, health-related offerings and other general insurance. ([GA Uganda product portfolio](https://www.gainsuranceltd.com/ug/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/Company-Profile-UG-2021.pdf), [ICEA LION Uganda](https://icealion.co.ug/), [AAR Uganda General Insurance products](https://aar-insurance.ug/general-insurance-products/))
- Official insurer product pages also show life and health variations beyond a single generic policy: Jubilee Uganda lists individual/family medical, senior medical, education, funeral, wealth protection, credit life, pensions and corporate health; Jubilee’s corporate health page specifies provider-network and plan-dependent benefits. ([Jubilee Uganda product catalog](https://jubileeinsurance.com/ug/), [Jubilee Corporate Health Cover](https://jubileeinsurance.com/ug/product/corporate-health-cover/))

### Inferences

- For a build/test program, the strongest “representative market coverage” starts with individual life, medical, comprehensive motor, fire/property and credit life, because these are the top six UIA-reported premium classes. This recommendation is about prioritizing the test corpus, not asserting that each insurer offers all six. ([UIA 2024 Integrated Report](https://uia.co.ug/sites/default/files/2025-06/UIA%20Annual%20Report%202024.pdf))
- Motor third-party deserves a separate wording from comprehensive motor because IRA and UIA describe materially different scope and obligations, and third-party-only motor is described by UIA as legally compulsory. ([IRA FAQ](https://ira.go.ug/frequently-asked-questions/), [UIA MTP FAQs](https://www.uia.co.ug/faq/mtp))
- Personal accident is a useful next test family: IRA reports it as a distinct, sizeable non-life class and insurer materials show benefits such as accidental death, disability, temporary disability, medical costs and funeral benefits that create meaningful extraction variation. ([IRA 2024 Annual Insurance Market Report](https://ira.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2024-Insurance-Industry-market-report.pdf), [ICEA LION Personal Accident brochure](https://icealion.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/ICEA-LION-UGANDA-PERSONAL-ACCIDENT-INSURANCE.pdf))
- Marine, engineering, travel and agriculture should be represented as a later extension or specialist pack, not omitted from the product taxonomy: the Insurance Act and the IRA report recognize these lines, and insurer catalogs show examples, but the UIA top-six list does not place them among the leading 2024 classes. ([Insurance Act](https://www.businesslicences.go.ug/uploads/documents/theinsuranceact-1.pdf), [IRA 2024 Annual Insurance Market Report](https://ira.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2024-Insurance-Industry-market-report.pdf), [GA product portfolio](https://www.gainsuranceltd.com/ug/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/Company-Profile-UG-2021.pdf))

### Gaps

- The public UIA ranking is not a distribution of insurers by product line. It cannot establish the median insurer’s exact menu, how many insurers sell each class, or which products are universal. ([UIA 2024 Integrated Report](https://uia.co.ug/sites/default/files/2025-06/UIA%20Annual%20Report%202024.pdf))
- The newer IRA 2025 annual report is listed by the regulator, but an insurer-by-insurer product offering matrix is not part of the evidence reviewed here; its aggregate non-life class totals and market taxonomy should be used to update the test shortlist if current class-level revenue rankings are required. ([IRA 2025 Annual Insurance Market Report](https://ira.go.ug/download/2025-insurance-industry-annual-market-report-2/))
- The annual industry report aggregates some categories (for example “Motor Insurance” and “Health and Medical”) that do not map one-to-one to retail product variants such as comprehensive vs third party, family vs corporate health, or individual vs group life. ([IRA 2024 Annual Insurance Market Report](https://ira.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2024-Insurance-Industry-market-report.pdf))
- A public, current, insurer-by-insurer matrix of active products and standard policy wording versions was not located in the sources reviewed; catalogs and brochures do not prove that a particular wording is current or that all terms are publicly downloadable. ([GA Uganda product portfolio](https://www.gainsuranceltd.com/ug/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/Company-Profile-UG-2021.pdf), [ICEA LION Uganda](https://icealion.co.ug/), [AAR Uganda General Insurance products](https://aar-insurance.ug/general-insurance-products/))

## Which policy wordings should IIP use first to exercise onboarding and extraction?

### Takeaway

Use a baseline that covers all six UIA-ranked classes from 2024: individual life, medical/health, comprehensive motor, fire/property, credit life, and a deposit-administered scheme rule pack. Add motor third-party as a separate seventh policy sample because its cover scope differs from comprehensive motor. Add personal accident next, then marine or engineering as a specialist-document challenge. ([UIA 2024 Integrated Report](https://uia.co.ug/sites/default/files/2025-06/UIA%20Annual%20Report%202024.pdf), [IRA 2024 Annual Insurance Market Report](https://ira.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2024-Insurance-Industry-market-report.pdf), [IRA FAQ](https://ira.go.ug/frequently-asked-questions/))

### Cited Findings

- UIA’s 2024 industry ranking puts individual life, medical, comprehensive motor and fire in the top four classes by written premium; credit life and deposit-administered schemes complete the top six. ([UIA 2024 Integrated Report](https://uia.co.ug/sites/default/files/2025-06/UIA%20Annual%20Report%202024.pdf))
- IRA’s statutory FAQ and UIA’s MTP FAQ distinguish third-party-only motor from wider own-damage covers, making them distinct extraction targets rather than interchangeable motor documents. ([IRA FAQ](https://ira.go.ug/frequently-asked-questions/), [UIA MTP FAQs](https://www.uia.co.ug/faq/mtp))
- Public insurer materials expose real variation in motor cover: ICEA LION describes comprehensive, third-party-only, third-party fire and theft, enhanced third-party and motorcycle categories; its motor brochure says comprehensive cover can address damage/loss to the insured vehicle and third-party liabilities. ([ICEA LION Motor Insurance brochure](https://icealion.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/ICEA-LION-UGANDA-MOTOR-INSURANCE.pdf))
- AAR’s fire description identifies property interests that can include buildings, furniture, contents and stock, and perils that include fire and allied perils such as lightning, storm, flood, malicious damage and impact by vehicles. ([AAR Uganda General Insurance products](https://aar-insurance.ug/general-insurance-products/))
- Jubilee’s Uganda catalog includes individual/family and corporate health, and its corporate plan describes plan options, provider networks, overseas referral, evacuation and stated waiting periods for selected conditions or tests. These features create useful varied fields for policy-terms extraction. ([Jubilee Uganda product catalog](https://jubileeinsurance.com/ug/), [Jubilee Corporate Health Cover](https://jubileeinsurance.com/ug/product/corporate-health-cover/))
- Jubilee Uganda lists credit life as a product, and IRA classifies “Group Credit” separately in life revenue statistics. ([Jubilee Uganda product catalog](https://jubileeinsurance.com/ug/), [IRA 2024 Annual Insurance Market Report](https://ira.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2024-Insurance-Industry-market-report.pdf))
- ICEA LION’s Uganda personal accident brochure distinguishes accidental death, permanent disability, temporary total disability, accidental medical expenses, funeral expenses and benefit limits. ([ICEA LION Personal Accident Insurance brochure](https://icealion.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/ICEA-LION-UGANDA-PERSONAL-ACCIDENT-INSURANCE.pdf))

### Inferences

- Recommended priority tiers:
  1. **Baseline policies:** comprehensive motor, motor third-party, fire/property, individual life, medical/health and credit life. The UIA 2024 top-six list supports all except the separate third-party variant, whose inclusion is an additional test-design choice because IRA describes it as a distinct statutory motor cover. ([UIA 2024 Integrated Report](https://uia.co.ug/sites/default/files/2025-06/UIA%20Annual%20Report%202024.pdf), [IRA FAQ](https://ira.go.ug/frequently-asked-questions/))
  2. **Baseline onboarding pack:** include deposit-administered scheme terms/configuration as a distinct non-claim policy/scheme document because it is also in UIA’s 2024 top six; validate that IIP does not misclassify it as a general claim wording. ([UIA 2024 Integrated Report](https://uia.co.ug/sites/default/files/2025-06/UIA%20Annual%20Report%202024.pdf))
  3. **Next:** personal accident and group life. Both are established classes in IRA reporting and public insurer catalogs, while personal accident wording offers benefit schedules and percentage/amount distinctions useful to extraction evaluation. ([IRA 2024 Annual Insurance Market Report](https://ira.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2024-Insurance-Industry-market-report.pdf), [ICEA LION Personal Accident brochure](https://icealion.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/ICEA-LION-UGANDA-PERSONAL-ACCIDENT-INSURANCE.pdf), [IRA FAQ](https://ira.go.ug/frequently-asked-questions/))
  3. **Specialist extension:** marine cargo and contractors’ all risks / engineering, followed by agriculture or travel if the intended customer mix includes those risks. IRA recognizes these business classes and GA’s portfolio provides market examples; however, the reviewed top-six market ranking does not identify them as leading classes. ([IRA 2024 Annual Insurance Market Report](https://ira.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2024-Insurance-Industry-market-report.pdf), [Insurance Act](https://www.businesslicences.go.ug/uploads/documents/theinsuranceact-1.pdf), [GA Uganda product portfolio](https://www.gainsuranceltd.com/ug/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/Company-Profile-UG-2021.pdf))
- For each class, use a policy pack rather than only a long narrative wording: a schedule/declarations page, main wording, endorsements/riders, and relevant proposal or benefit schedule. This is a test-design inference from the insurer materials’ explicit use of schedules, plan choices, benefit limits and options; the reviewed sources do not publish a universal Uganda-standard document bundle. ([ICEA LION Personal Accident brochure](https://icealion.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/ICEA-LION-UGANDA-PERSONAL-ACCIDENT-INSURANCE.pdf), [Jubilee Corporate Health Cover](https://jubileeinsurance.com/ug/product/corporate-health-cover/), [ICEA LION Motor Insurance brochure](https://icealion.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/ICEA-LION-UGANDA-MOTOR-INSURANCE.pdf))
- Across those packs, design extraction checks for insurer/product identity, policy number, insured/proposer, beneficiaries or covered members, risk/asset identifiers, cover start/end dates, premium and payment frequency, sums insured/limits, sublimits, excess/deductible, covered events, exclusions, conditions/warranties, waiting periods, endorsements and claim notification requirements. This is a proposed IIP field checklist based on the types of cover, limits, parties, plan options and exclusions described in the cited insurer materials; it is not presented as a regulator-mandated common schema. ([ICEA LION Motor Insurance brochure](https://icealion.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/ICEA-LION-UGANDA-MOTOR-INSURANCE.pdf), [ICEA LION Personal Accident brochure](https://icealion.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/ICEA-LION-UGANDA-PERSONAL-ACCIDENT-INSURANCE.pdf), [AAR Fire Products](https://aar-insurance.ug/general-insurance-products/), [Jubilee Corporate Health Cover](https://jubileeinsurance.com/ug/product/corporate-health-cover/))

### Gaps

- Public marketing pages and brochures may not expose the complete operative contract, proposal form, schedule layout, definitions, exclusions, endorsements or claim clauses needed to mirror an insurer’s production onboarding. The insurer should provide approved current samples before live configuration. ([ICEA LION Motor Insurance brochure](https://icealion.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/ICEA-LION-UGANDA-MOTOR-INSURANCE.pdf), [ICEA LION Personal Accident brochure](https://icealion.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/ICEA-LION-UGANDA-PERSONAL-ACCIDENT-INSURANCE.pdf), [AAR Uganda General Insurance products](https://aar-insurance.ug/general-insurance-products/))
- The sources do not establish one standard wording, field schema, or claims-document requirement across insurers; policy terms and document requests need to be tenant-configurable and grounded in the insurer’s own approved wording. ([IRA FAQ](https://ira.go.ug/frequently-asked-questions/), [UIA Non-Life FAQs](https://www.uia.co.ug/non-life-faqs))

## What should the onboarding test corpus prove about extraction and product configuration?

### Takeaway

The test should prove IIP can distinguish product class and sub-type, extract values from both structured schedules and narrative clauses, connect endorsements to the correct base policy, preserve evidence citations, and surface ambiguity for human confirmation. The chosen classes deliberately exercise people, vehicles, property, health benefits and lender-linked cover rather than repeating one document layout. ([UIA 2024 Integrated Report](https://uia.co.ug/sites/default/files/2025-06/UIA%20Annual%20Report%202024.pdf), [ICEA LION Motor Insurance brochure](https://icealion.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/ICEA-LION-UGANDA-MOTOR-INSURANCE.pdf), [Jubilee Corporate Health Cover](https://jubileeinsurance.com/ug/product/corporate-health-cover/))

### Cited Findings

- Motor product sources distinguish private/commercial/motorcycle uses and cover sub-types, while the IRA identifies materially different motor protection tiers. ([ICEA LION Motor Insurance brochure](https://icealion.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/ICEA-LION-UGANDA-MOTOR-INSURANCE.pdf), [IRA FAQ](https://ira.go.ug/frequently-asked-questions/))
- Property/fire sources describe multiple insured interests (building, contents, stock) and multiple perils rather than a single generic insured object. ([AAR Uganda Fire Products](https://aar-insurance.ug/general-insurance-products/))
- Health and personal accident sources describe multiple benefit categories, options, limits, waiting periods and covered groups, all of which vary by product/plan. ([Jubilee Corporate Health Cover](https://jubileeinsurance.com/ug/product/corporate-health-cover/), [ICEA LION Personal Accident brochure](https://icealion.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/ICEA-LION-UGANDA-PERSONAL-ACCIDENT-INSURANCE.pdf))
- Uganda’s Insurance Act recognizes a broad taxonomy of life and non-life classes; IRA’s annual report separately reports multiple class lines, so IIP onboarding should not collapse every document into a generic “policy” profile. ([Insurance Act](https://www.businesslicences.go.ug/uploads/documents/theinsuranceact-1.pdf), [IRA 2024 Annual Insurance Market Report](https://ira.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2024-Insurance-Industry-market-report.pdf))

### Inferences

- Include clean examples and paired challenge versions for each baseline class: (a) schedule and wording disagree on one key value; (b) endorsement changes a limit, covered person, vehicle or risk address; (c) values appear in tables, prose, headers or scanned pages; (d) optional benefits are selected vs merely described; and (e) a missing value must remain unknown rather than be invented. These are recommended test cases inferred from the variation in source materials’ schedules, selected plan options, limits and endorsements; they are not reported insurer-wide defect rates. ([ICEA LION Motor Insurance brochure](https://icealion.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/ICEA-LION-UGANDA-MOTOR-INSURANCE.pdf), [ICEA LION Personal Accident brochure](https://icealion.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/ICEA-LION-UGANDA-PERSONAL-ACCIDENT-INSURANCE.pdf), [Jubilee Corporate Health Cover](https://jubileeinsurance.com/ug/product/corporate-health-cover/))
- For accuracy scoring, maintain a gold-label field set with source page/section and evidence quote per field, then score product classification, exact field value, units/currency/date normalization, endorsement precedence, citation location and abstention on absent/contradictory data. This is a proposed evaluation method inferred from the stated onboarding extraction objective; the cited sources support the document variation, not this scoring method. ([IRA 2024 Annual Insurance Market Report](https://ira.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2024-Insurance-Industry-market-report.pdf), [ICEA LION Motor Insurance brochure](https://icealion.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/ICEA-LION-UGANDA-MOTOR-INSURANCE.pdf), [Jubilee Corporate Health Cover](https://jubileeinsurance.com/ug/product/corporate-health-cover/))
- Generate only fictional insurer names, people, contacts, policy IDs, vehicle registrations, bank/ID numbers and claims evidence for onboarding tests; this is a safety recommendation for synthetic test evidence and is not a claim about market practice.

### Gaps

- This research did not inspect IIP’s current onboarding parser, schema, supported file types, citation behavior or acceptance criteria; those need a separate repository review before turning this shortlist into a precise implementation test plan.
- No public insurer-wide machine-readable catalogue of approved policy terms or standard schema was found in the reviewed primary sources; product wording and onboarding rule extraction therefore need insurer-specific approval and version control. ([IRA 2024 Annual Insurance Market Report](https://ira.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2024-Insurance-Industry-market-report.pdf), [UIA 2024 Integrated Report](https://uia.co.ug/sites/default/files/2025-06/UIA%20Annual%20Report%202024.pdf))
